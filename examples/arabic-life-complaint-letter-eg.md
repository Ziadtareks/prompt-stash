## Prompt (filled)

> You are a consumer-rights assistant. Draft my complaint in formal Arabic (with an English version option) that states facts, cites my attempts, and names the resolution I expect.
>
> MY DETAILS
> - Provider and service: WE home internet
> - What went wrong and since when: outage 12 days, 3 tickets since June 1
> - What I already tried: call center x4, tech visit promised twice, no-show
> - The resolution I want: fix within 72 hours or cancel contract penalty-free + refund the outage days
> - My account details: account 0123456789

**2. THE ENGLISH VERSION**

> Subject: Formal complaint — 12-day service outage, account 0123456789
>
> Dear Customer Affairs Manager,
>
> I am writing regarding persistent failure to restore my WE home internet service (account **0123456789**). The service has been down for **12 consecutive days since Sunday, June 1**. During this period I filed **3 fault tickets** and made **4 recorded calls** to your call center. Two technician visits were scheduled and confirmed; **no technician arrived** on either occasion.
>
> The outage has caused me direct, ongoing harm: I work remotely and have had to pay for a mobile-data backup for the entire period.
>
> I therefore request, explicitly: **(1)** full restoration of service within **72 hours** of receipt of this letter, **or (2)** penalty-free contract cancellation with a refund for the 12 outage days from the current billing cycle.
>
> Attached: the three ticket references, dated speed-test screenshots, and the latest payment receipt. Should I receive no resolution within 72 hours, I will escalate through your formal complaints channel and then to the competent national authorities.
>
> Sincerely, [Name] — [Phone] — [Date]

**3. THE ESCALATION NOTE**

If the provider ignores the 72-hour window: (a) file through the provider's own formal written-complaints channel — this creates the paper trail regulators ask for; (b) escalate to the **national consumer protection body** for service-failure complaints, and to the **telecom regulator** for connection services. I leave you the current portal links to fill in yourself — they change and I won't invent them.

**4. THE EVIDENCE PACK**

- Photos/screenshot of the router's red status light, with date visible.
- Speed-test screenshots (0 Mbps) taken every few days — dates on them.
- The 3 ticket numbers + call dates, in a one-line log: `June 1 — ticket #A — no fix · June 4 — call, promised visit June 6 — no-show · June 9 — visit 2 no-show …`
- Latest payment receipt showing the paid subscription period.

**5. THE SOCIAL OPTION** (only after the 72 hours pass)

> 12 days without internet, 3 tickets, 4 calls, 2 missed technician appointments — and still counting. Account 0123456789. All documented. I'd rather solve this with the company than in public — 72 hours left on my formal deadline. @WE
