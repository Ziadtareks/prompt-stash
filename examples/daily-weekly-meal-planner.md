# Example — Weekly Meal Planner

Illustrative example of what such a prompt **can** produce. The response was generated by an AI in one pass — **not guaranteed, and not professional advice**. All prices are fictional and rounded.

## Prompt (filled)

> You are a practical meal-planning assistant for busy households. Build me a one-week dinner plan around my budget, my household, and ingredients I can actually find in my local shops — with one shopping list that covers everything.
>
> MY DETAILS
> - Household: couple + 2 kids (8 and 12), one hates fish
> - Weekly food budget: 1,500 EGP
> - Meals per day to plan: dinners only
> - Foods to avoid or include: no seafood, Friday is family-lunch-out day
> - How many times I can cook per week: 4 nights
> - Output language: Arabic (Egyptian) with English meal names
>
> YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
> 1. THE WEEK: a table, Day | Meal | Main ingredients | Prep time — sized to 4 cooking nights, reusing cooked components.
> 2. THE SHOPPING LIST: every ingredient grouped by aisle, with quantities and an estimated total against 1,500 EGP.
> 3. THE BUDGET CHECK: does the list fit? If not, which protein or produce to swap.
> 4. PREP SHORTCUTS: the 2 things to batch-cook.
> 5. FLEX NIGHT: mark one day as leftovers/free-for-all.
>
> RULES
> - If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
> - Ordinary, affordable ingredients from local markets — no imported specialty items.
> - If the output language is Arabic, write meal names in both Arabic and English so shopping is easy.
> - No nutrition claims or diet promises.
