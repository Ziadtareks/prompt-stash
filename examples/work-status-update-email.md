## Prompt (filled)

>

> - Project or workstream name: company website migration to new hosting
> - Reporting period: week of Oct 5-9
> - My raw notes — progress, blockers, next steps, unpolished: DNS switched Tuesday, old site still up as backup; checkout page retest at 40% success rate, bug ticket filed; waiting on hosting support since Thursday; plan: finish retest next week
> - Tone (neutral professional, upbeat, formal): neutral professional
> - Output language (English, Arabic, or both side by side): English
>
> YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
> *(the prompt's six ordered sections — subject line, TL;DR, progress, risks & blockers, next period, full email)*
>
> RULES
> *(use only facts from my notes, [FILL] markers for anything missing, no corporate padding, say plainly if something is late)*
