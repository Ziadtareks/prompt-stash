# Example — WhatsApp Broadcast Writer

Demonstration of [`prompts/marketing/whatsapp-broadcast.txt`](../prompts/marketing/whatsapp-broadcast.txt) with the variables filled in. Illustrative example of what such a prompt **can** produce. The response was generated by an AI in one pass — **not guaranteed, and not professional advice**. All names, numbers, and details are fictional (Egypt-plausible where relevant).

## Prompt (filled)

> You are a WhatsApp marketing specialist for small businesses. Write broadcast messages that feel personal, get read, and don't trip spam instincts.
>
> MY DETAILS
> - My business and what I'm announcing: Sweet Meter — winter menu launched (6 new desserts)
> - Who receives it (past buyers, warm leads, group members): past buyers on my broadcast list
> - The action I want (order, reply, book, visit): reply to pre-order for next weekend
> - Offer details and deadline if any: first 30 orders get a free mini cheesecake; closes Thursday
> - Language and tone: Egyptian Arabic, friendly
>
> YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
> 1. THE MESSAGE: max 80 words, opening with something personal/relevant (no "Dear customer"), the news, the reply to pre-order for next weekend with a clear how (reply, link, visit), and the deadline.
> 2. THE STATUS VERSION: the same offer as a WhatsApp Status — under 40 words + emoji, made for swipers.
> 3. THE FOLLOW-UP: the one polite reminder 2 days before the deadline, for people who didn't act.
> 4. SEGMENTATION NOTE: which recipients should NOT get this blast (bought yesterday, asked to stop) — list hygiene beats volume.
> 5. ANTI-BLOCK RULES: the do/don't list that keeps my number out of "reported for spam" (no mass forwards, personal greeting where possible, easy opt-out).
>
> RULES
> - If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
> - It must read like a message from a person, not a flyer — verbs and specifics, no logo-speak.
> - One message = one action; two CTAs halve the response.
> - Local timing note (Egypt/Gulf) for the best send window.
>
> If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.
>
> OUTPUT FORMAT
> - Five numbered sections: message (≤80 words), status version (≤40 words), follow-up (1 message), segmentation exclusions, anti-block do/don't list. Lead with THE MESSAGE.
>

## Example AI response

**1. THE MESSAGE**

> أهلاً بيكم تاني 👋
> القائمة الشتوية الجديدة عند Sweet Meter نزلت — 6 حلويات جديدة، منهم تشيز كيك اللوتس البارد وسينابون التمر 🍫
> عارفين إن الشتا بتحب الحاجات الساخنة الحلوة، فجربوا الساخن منهم على طول.
> بنستقبل طلبات نهاية الأسبوع بالرد على الرسالة دي، وأول 30 طلب معاهم ميني تشيز كيك هدية 🎁
> آخر يوم للعرض الخميس.

**2. THE STATUS VERSION**

> القائمة الشتوية وصلت 🍫✨
> 6 حلويات جديدة — تشيز كيك لوتس وسينابون تمر وأكتر
> أول 30 طلب معاهم هدية 🎁 اطلب قبل الخميس

**3. THE FOLLOW-UP** (Tuesday)

> لسه بتفكر في الحلو؟ 😄
> عرض القائمة الشتوية بيقفل الخميس — أول 30 طلب معاهم ميني تشيز كيك هدية 🎁
> ابعت اسم الحلو والكمية في رسالة وهنأكدلك على طول.

**4. SEGMENTATION NOTE**
Exclude three groups: anyone who ordered in the last 7 days (they already know the new menu — send them a thank-you instead), anyone who ever asked to stop receiving messages, and contacts with no order in 6+ months — send those a separate one-line "still want to hear from us?" ping before ever promoting again.

**5. ANTI-BLOCK RULES**
- DO: message only past buyers; greet personally where your tool allows names; keep one clear action per message; honor opt-outs the same day; send 10am–10pm local time.
- DON'T: forward the identical text to groups and broadcast lists on the same day, attach more than 2 photos, keep promoting non-responders more than twice a month, or send again after a "stop" — one report from a customer flags your whole number.
