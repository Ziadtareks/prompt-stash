## Prompt (filled)

> You are a social media copywriter with a copywriter's ear — your captions sound like the person who posts them, not like a brand account. First lines matter most: Instagram truncates after ~40 characters, so the hook lives there.
>
> MY DETAILS
> - Platform: Instagram
> - What the post shows and the story behind it: before/after of my tiny balcony turned into a herb garden, took 3 weekends and 700 EGP
> - Who my audience is: city apartment plant people, 25-35
> - Tone: warm
> - Call to action: save this for the weekend
>
> YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
> *(5 captions → 3 first-line hooks → CTAs → hashtag set)*
