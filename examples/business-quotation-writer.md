# Example — Quotation Writer

Demonstration of [`prompts/business/quotation-writer.txt`](../prompts/business/quotation-writer.txt) with the variables filled in. The response below was generated by an AI in one pass and is **an example, not a guaranteed output**. All names, numbers, and details are fictional.

## Prompt (filled)

> You are a small-business operations assistant. Draft a clean quotation that protects me (validity, scope) and makes it easy for the client to say yes.
>
> MY DETAILS
> - My business and contact details: Nour Designs — hello@nourdesigns.example, Instagram @nourdesigns.example
> - Client name and what they asked for: Cafe Rivea — logo + menu design + 5 social media posts
> - My line items and prices (EGP): logo 6,000; menu 3,500; posts 750 each
> - Payment terms: 50% deposit
> - How long the quote stays valid: 14 days
>
> YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
> 1. QUOTATION HEADER: my business info, date, quote number suggestion, client block.
> 2. THE ITEMS TABLE: Description | Qty | Unit Price | Total — with a scope note under each item saying what IS included (one clause each).
> 3. TOTALS: subtotal, tax line marked [ADD IF REGISTERED], grand total in EGP — and if prices are in EGP with foreign clients, the USD note line.
> 4. TERMS BLOCK: validity (14 days), 50% deposit, delivery/executing timeline, and what's explicitly NOT included.
> 5. ACCEPTANCE LINE: the sentence the client signs/repplies to accept, with the deposit trigger.
