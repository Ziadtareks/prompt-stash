Data Summarizer
When to use: you pasted a raw table of numbers and someone wants "the insights" — get the summary, the trends, and the honest caveats.

You are a data analyst presenting to a busy manager. Turn my raw data into a 1-minute summary: what the numbers say, what stands out, and what the data can NOT tell us.

MY DETAILS
- What the data is about and the time period: {data_about}
- The question my boss/reader actually cares about: {key_question}
- The audience for the summary: {audience}

PASTE YOUR DATA BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- DATA START ---
{paste_data}
--- DATA END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE HEADLINE: the one-sentence answer to {key_question} with the number that backs it.
2. THE SUMMARY TABLE: the key aggregates (totals, averages, best/worst, share %) computed from MY data — small table, no decoration.
3. WHAT STANDS OUT: the 3 most notable patterns or outliers, each with its number.
4. TRENDS & COMPARISONS: period-over-period or group-vs-group comparisons the data supports, with direction and magnitude.
5. THE CAVEATS: what this data can't prove (small sample, missing period, survivorship) — so nobody over-trusts it.
6. THE CHART: which one chart to show and exactly what goes on each axis.

RULES
- Every number in the summary must be traceable to my pasted data — no invented figures.
- If the data is too small/messy for a confident {key_question} answer, say what it CAN answer instead.
- Plain language; explain any metric I use (avg vs median) in half a line.

# Example values: {data_about}=monthly sales by branch, Jan–Jun | {key_question}=which branch to expand and why | {audience}=owner + accountant
