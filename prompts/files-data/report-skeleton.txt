Report Skeleton Builder
When to use it: you have raw numbers and a monthly/weekly report due — build the report structure once, then it's fill-in-the-blanks forever.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: reporting, business

You are a business reporting assistant. Design my report skeleton: sections in the order decision-makers read, placeholder charts, and the "so what" lines that make numbers mean something.

MY DETAILS
- Report type and frequency (monthly sales, weekly ops, project status): {report_type}
- Who reads it and what decisions they make with it: {readers}
- The data I have available each period: {available_data}
- Format (email, slide, one-pager): {format}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE STRUCTURE: sections in reading order — verdict first, then details — with a one-line purpose per section.
2. THE METRICS TABLE: the 5–7 numbers that matter for this {report_type} — Metric | This period | Last period | Change | Target — the same row set every period.
3. THE NARRATIVE SLOTS: for each section, the sentence pattern to fill ("Revenue was X, up/down Y% because Z") — reports fail when numbers arrive without the "because".
4. THE RED-FLAG SECTION: where to put problems/risks honestly (not buried), with the format: what, impact, my action, help needed.
5. THE TEMPLATE: the whole skeleton assembled in {format} layout, ready to save and refill each period.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- The verdict/summary goes first — readers who stop at line 3 still get the point.
- Same metrics every period; trends need consistency, not novelty.
- Every number slot pairs with a "why" slot — unexplained numbers invite wrong conclusions.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: reading-order structure, metrics table (5-7 rows: Metric | This period | Last | Change | Target), narrative sentence patterns, red-flag section format, the assembled template. Lead with THE STRUCTURE.

# Variables
- {report_type}: required, report type + frequency, e.g. "monthly tutoring-center report"
- {readers}: required, who reads the report and what they decide
- {available_data}: required, data you have each period, e.g. "enrollments, revenue, attendance"
- {format}: required, output format, e.g. "one-page email" or "slide"

# Example values: {report_type}=monthly tutoring-center report | {readers}=owner (me) + the two investors | {available_data}=enrollments, revenue EGP, attendance %, cancellations, ad spend | {format}=one-page email
