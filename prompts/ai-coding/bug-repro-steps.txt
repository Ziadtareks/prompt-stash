Bug Repro Builder
When to use it: someone (or you, last week) reported a bug in one vague sentence, and "it doesn't work" is not something you can fix.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: debugging, qa
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact secrets and tokens before pasting.

You are a QA engineer. Turn my vague bug report into a minimal reproduction recipe — the exact steps that make the bug happen every time.

MY DETAILS
- The vague report: {vague_report}
- Where it happens (page, endpoint, device, environment): {where}
- What I know about the reporter's actions: {context}
- What "working" looks like: {expected_behavior}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. QUESTIONS FIRST: the 3 questions whose answers would cut the search space in half (only if the answer truly isn't in my inputs).
2. THE HYPOTHESES: 3 candidate causes, each with a one-line "how to trigger it on purpose".
3. REPRO DRAFT: step-by-step recipe for the most likely hypothesis — numbered, exact clicks/requests, with the expected result at each step and the point where it diverges from {expected_behavior}.
4. MINIMIZE: which steps to strip away once reproduced, to reach the smallest failing case (the one you'd attach to a ticket).
5. THE TICKET: title + body ready to paste — observed vs expected, environment, repro steps, frequency.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- A repro that fails 50% of the time is a hypothesis, not a repro — label confidence honestly.
- Never invent user actions I wasn't told about; mark assumptions with [ASSUMED].
- Environment details (browser, OS, account state) are mandatory fields — flag them if missing.
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: ≤3 clarifying questions (only if needed), 3 hypotheses with trigger hints, numbered repro recipe with expected-vs-actual at the divergence, minimization notes, ticket-ready title + body. Lead with QUESTIONS or HYPOTHESES.
