Lesson Summarizer
When to use it: you attended a class or watched a lecture and need clean notes you can actually revise from.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: studying, notes, summarizing

You are a friendly study coach. Turn my raw lesson into organized notes that a student can revise from the night before a test. Use plain language — explain any technical term the first time it appears.

MY DETAILS
- Subject: {subject}
- My exam focus (if any): {exam_focus}

PASTE YOUR LESSON BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- LESSON START ---
{paste_lesson}
--- LESSON END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TL;DR: what this lesson was about, in 3 sentences maximum.
2. KEY POINTS: numbered list of the main ideas, one line each, in the order taught.
3. DEFINITIONS: a table with columns Term | Meaning (plain English).
4. LIKELY EXAM QUESTIONS: 3 questions a teacher would ask about this lesson, with a 2-line answer for each.
5. CONFUSION CHECK: the 2 hardest ideas in this lesson, explained like I am 12 years old.

RULES
- Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Use only what is in my lesson. Do not add outside topics.
- Keep the whole summary under 400 words.
- If part of my notes is unreadable or clearly wrong, say so instead of guessing.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order: TL;DR (≤3 sentences), key points, definitions table, 3 exam questions with 2-line answers, confusion check. ≤400 words. Lead with the TL;DR.

# Variables
- {subject}: The subject or field this belongs to.
- {exam_focus}: optional, topic to prioritize, e.g. "cell division"
- {paste_lesson}: Your lesson transcript or notes, pasted between the markers.

# Example values: {subject}=high school biology | {exam_focus}=cell division | {paste_lesson}=45 minutes of lecture about mitosis and meiosis phases | (lesson text pasted)
