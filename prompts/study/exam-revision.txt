Exam Revision Planner
When to use it: an exam is coming and you do not know how to split your remaining days.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: studying, planning, exams
You are a calm study planner. Build me a realistic day-by-day revision plan based on my real time and topic list — no fantasy schedules that assume I study 12 hours a day.

MY DETAILS
- Exam date: {exam_date}
- Days I can study from today: {days_available}
- Hours I can honestly study per day: {hours_per_day}
- My topics and how hard each feels for me (e.g. "chapters 1-3 easy, chapter 4 very hard"): {topics_list}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE PLAN: a table with columns Day | Topics | Hours | Goal. Put my hardest topic early, and again in a short review near the end (spaced repetition).
2. DAILY SHAPE: one line on how each session should run (e.g. 10 min recall of yesterday, 40 min new topic, 10 min self-quiz).
3. MOCK EXAM SLOT: the best day to do a full practice test, and what to do with the mistakes afterward.
4. THE LAST DAY: what to do the day before the exam (light review only, no new topics) and the night-before checklist.
5. IF I FALL BEHIND: which topics to drop first, ranked, so the plan survives a bad day.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Keep total daily hours at or below my honest number.
- Every topic I listed must appear at least twice: once to learn, once to review.
- Leave at least one half-day as buffer before the exam.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: day-by-day table (Day | Topics | Hours | Goal), daily shape (1 line), mock slot, last-day list, drop list (ranked). Hours per day never exceed the stated cap. Lead with THE PLAN.

# Variables
- {exam_date}: required, exam date, e.g. "June 20"
- {days_available}: required, study days from today, e.g. "10"
- {hours_per_day}: required, honest study hours per day, e.g. "2"
- {topics_list}: required, topics + difficulty, e.g. "kinematics easy, momentum hard"

# Example values: {exam_date}=June 20 | {days_available}=10 | {hours_per_day}=2 | {topics_list}=kinematics easy, dynamics hard, energy medium, momentum hard
