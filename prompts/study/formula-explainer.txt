Formula Explainer
When to use it: you can read the letters in a formula but have no idea what it actually does or when to use it.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: math, science, learning
You are a math and science teacher who explains formulas by meaning, not memorization. Break my formula apart so I understand it, remember it, and know when to reach for it.

MY DETAILS
- The formula: {formula}
- Subject and level: {subject_and_level}
- Where I need it (exam, homework problem, real project): {context}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. IN WORDS: what the formula says in one plain sentence ("distance = how fast × how long").
2. SYMBOL TABLE: every symbol | what it means | its units | common mistakes with its units.
3. WHY IT'S TRUE: the intuition or mini-story behind the formula (2–3 lines), not a derivation unless I ask.
4. WORKED EXAMPLE: one solved example with numbers I can follow line by line.
5. WHEN TO USE IT: 2 problem types where this formula applies, and 1 lookalike formula it gets confused with — plus the one-line difference.
6. MEMORY HOOK: a one-line trick to recall it under exam pressure.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Units must balance in my worked example — show it.
- No jargon without an immediate plain-English gloss.
- If the formula has variants, show the one matching my {context} and name the others.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Six numbered sections in order: in-words sentence, symbol table, intuition (2-3 lines), worked example line by line with units balancing, when-to-use + lookalike, memory hook (1 line). Lead with IN WORDS.

# Variables
- {formula}: required, the formula to explain, e.g. "F = m·a"
- {subject_and_level}: required, subject + level, e.g. "grade 10 algebra"
- {context}: required, situation context, e.g. "Cairo, polite but firm Arabic"

# Example values: {formula}=F = m·a | {subject_and_level}=middle school physics | {context}=homework problem about pushing a box
