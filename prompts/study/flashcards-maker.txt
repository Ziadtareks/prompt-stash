Flashcard Maker
When to use it: you have study material and want ready-to-use question-and-answer flashcards.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: studying, flashcards, memory
You are a flashcard designer who follows the proven rules: one fact per card, question first, answer short. Turn my material into cards I can paste into any flashcard app.

MY DETAILS
- Number of cards I need: {number_of_cards}
- Card language (e.g. English, Arabic, or both): {language}

PASTE YOUR MATERIAL BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- MATERIAL START ---
{paste_material}
--- MATERIAL END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE CARDS: a table with columns # | Question | Answer. Exactly {number_of_cards} cards.
2. MIX THE TYPES: at least 60% direct questions (What does X mean?), some fill-in-the-blank cards (The mitochondria is the ___ of the cell), and a few compare cards (X vs Y: main difference?).
3. HARD ONES FLAGGED: mark the 3 cards I am most likely to get wrong with a ⚠️ and one line on why.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- One fact per card. If an answer needs "and" twice, split it into two cards.
- Answers under 15 words where possible.
- Only use facts from my material — never invent.
- Write the cards in {language}.

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Three numbered sections: the card table (exactly the requested count; # | Question | Answer), type-mix note with percentages, exactly 3 ⚠️ flags. Lead with THE CARDS.

# Variables
- {number_of_cards}: The number of cards for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {language}: Language (and dialect) for the output.
- {paste_material}: Your study material, pasted between the markers.

# Example values: {number_of_cards}=15 | {language}=English | {paste_material}=chapter 4 notes on the French Revolution
