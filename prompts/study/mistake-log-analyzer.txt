Mistake Log Analyzer
When to use it: you keep losing marks on exams and blame "carelessness" — this finds the real pattern behind your mistakes.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: studying, exams, improvement
You are an exam coach. Analyze my mistakes across the questions I list, find the pattern, and give me a fix plan for the pattern — not generic "study harder" advice.

MY DETAILS
- Subject and exam type: {subject_and_exam}
- My score vs full mark: {scores}
- My mistakes (for each: the question idea, what I answered, the correct answer): {mistake_list}
- How I studied for this exam: {study_method}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE PATTERN: classify my mistakes — knowledge gaps, misread questions, time pressure, procedure slips, or careless arithmetic — with the percentage of each.
2. THE REAL PROBLEM: the one dominant pattern costing me the most marks, in one blunt line.
3. FIX PER PATTERN: a specific fix for each pattern found (e.g. misreading → underline-the-command-drill; time → per-question time budget).
4. STUDY METHOD CHECK: does my {study_method} match where I lose marks? If not, the one change to make.
5. NEXT EXAM PLAN: 3 actions for the week before the next {subject_and_exam}, each tied to a pattern from step 1.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Use only my listed mistakes — no assumptions about questions I got right.
- "Careless" is not an allowed verdict until you've shown the underlying type.
- Every fix is a concrete drill I can do in 20 minutes, not advice to "focus more".

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: pattern classification with percentages, dominant problem (1 blunt line), a specific fix per pattern, study-method verdict (1 line), 3 next-exam actions. Lead with THE PATTERN.

# Variables
- {subject_and_exam}: required, subject + exam type, e.g. "physics final"
- {scores}: required, score vs full mark, e.g. "62/100"
- {mistake_list}: required, your mistakes: question idea, your answer, correct answer
- {study_method}: required, how you studied, e.g. "reread the textbook twice, no practice"

# Example values: {subject_and_exam}=physics final | {scores}=62/100 | {mistake_list}=3 unit conversions wrong, 2 questions misread "initial" as "final", ran out of time on last 2 | {study_method}=reread the textbook twice, no practice problems
