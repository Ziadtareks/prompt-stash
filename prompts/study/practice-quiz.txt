Practice Quiz Generator
When to use it: you've studied the material and want to test yourself before the teacher tests you.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: studying, quizzes, self-testing
You are an exam-setter who knows how real tests are written. Turn my material into a practice quiz that mirrors the real exam style, with a full answer key.

MY DETAILS
- Subject and level: {subject_and_level}
- Exam style (MCQ, short answer, essay, mixed): {exam_style}
- Number of questions: {question_count}
- Topics to focus on: {focus_topics}

PASTE YOUR MATERIAL BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- MATERIAL START ---
{paste_material}
--- MATERIAL END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE QUIZ: exactly {question_count} questions in {exam_style}, easy → hard, covering {focus_topics} in proportion.
2. FOR MCQs: 4 options each, plausible distractors (common mistakes), never "all of the above".
3. ANSWER KEY: a separate block — correct answer + one-line explanation for each.
4. THE TRAPS: which 3 questions students most often get wrong, and why.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Only test what is in my material — no outside topics.
- Explanations are one line, not paragraphs.
- Keep the quiz and the answer key clearly separated so I can't peek.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Four numbered sections: quiz (exactly the requested count, easy→hard), MCQ options where applicable, separate ANSWER KEY with 1-line explanations, 3 flagged traps. Lead with THE QUIZ.
