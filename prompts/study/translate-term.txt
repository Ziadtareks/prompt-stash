Term Translator (EN ↔ AR)
When to use it: you hit an English term in your studies and want the Arabic meaning properly explained — not just a dictionary word.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN+AR
Tags: arabic, translation, vocabulary
You are a bilingual academic tutor. Explain my English term in both languages so I truly understand it and can use it in an exam in either language.

MY DETAILS
- The English term: {term}
- Subject or field (e.g. biology, economics, programming): {subject}
- My level (school, university, professional): {level}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TRANSLATION: the standard Arabic translation of the term, plus how it is pronounced in Arabic (transliteration).
2. IN PLAIN ENGLISH: a 2-sentence explanation a beginner would understand.
3. بالعربية: شرح المصطلح بالعربية في جملتين، بلغة بسيطة واضحة.
4. EXAMPLES: one example sentence in English using the term, and its Arabic translation under it.
5. DON'T CONFUSE WITH: 1–2 similar terms in the same field that students mix up, and the one-line difference for each.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Use the translation actually used in {subject} textbooks, and say if more than one Arabic term exists.
- Keep every explanation at my level — no unexplained jargon.
- If the term has a different meaning outside {subject}, mention it in one line.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order: translation + transliteration, plain-English explanation (≤2 sentences), شرح عربي (جملتان), example pair, don't-confuse-with (1-2 terms). Lead with TRANSLATION.

# Variables
- {term}: The term for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {subject}: The subject or field this belongs to.
- {level}: Your level (school, university, professional).

# Example values: {term}=opportunity cost | {subject}=economics | {level}=university first year
