Customer Persona Builder
When to use it: you're marketing to "everyone" and it's not working — define the one person you're actually selling to.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: research, positioning, marketing
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact personal or client data before pasting.

You are a market researcher. Build a concrete customer persona for my product based on my real observations — specific enough to write an ad to, not a demographic card.

MY DETAILS
- What I sell (and price): {product_and_price}
- Who actually buys today (or who I think will): {who_buys}
- What they tell me (reviews, DMs, objections I hear): {voice_of_customer}
- Where they spend time online and offline: {channels}
- Market (Egypt city / Gulf / regional): {market}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE PERSONA: a named profile — age, job, income bracket in local currency, family situation, the moment their problem shows up.
2. THE JOB-TO-BE-DONE: what they're really hiring my product to do (the progress they want), in their words from {voice_of_customer}.
3. TRIGGERS & BARRIERS: what makes them buy now (trigger events) and the 3 objections that stop them (price, trust, habit) — each with the counter-message.
4. WHERE TO REACH THEM: ranked channels for my {market} with the content format that works on each.
5. MESSAGE FIT: the one-line marketing message for this persona, and the words they actually use (steal from {voice_of_customer}).

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Built from my {voice_of_customer} evidence, not stock-persona clichés ("tech-savvy millennial" banned).
- One primary persona; if I'm serving two very different buyers, say so and pick the profitable one to start.
- Income/prices in local currency (EGP where my market is Egypt).
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: named persona, job-to-be-done in customer words, triggers + 3 objections with counter-messages, ranked channels, one-line message using their vocabulary. Lead with THE PERSONA.

# Variables
- {product_and_price}: required, product/service + price
- {who_buys}: required, who actually buys today, e.g. "working moms 30-45"
- {voice_of_customer}: required, what customers actually say, e.g. review and DM quotes
- {channels}: required, where word of mouth happens today, e.g. "parent WhatsApp groups"
- {market}: required, market/currency context, e.g. "EGP, Cairo fintech market"

# Example values: {product_and_price}=home organizing service, from 1,500 EGP/visit | {who_buys}=working moms 30-45, new apartments | {voice_of_customer}="I buy things twice because I can't find anything", clutter stress | {channels}=Instagram, mom Facebook groups | {market}=New Cairo
