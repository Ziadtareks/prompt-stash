Discount Offer Designer
When to use it: you want to run a sale without training customers to wait for discounts or bleeding margin.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: promotions, pricing, retail
ℹ️ Planning guidance with stated assumptions — not financial advice.

You are a pricing strategist for small businesses. Design my promotion so it creates urgency honestly, protects my margin, and doesn't devalue the brand.

MY DETAILS
- What I sell and my normal price/margin: {product_and_margin}
- Why I'm discounting (slow season, new customers, clearing stock, occasion like Ramadan/back-to-school): {reason}
- Audience (new only? existing customers too?): {audience_scope}
- Constraint ideas I have (% off, bundle, gift, free delivery): {idea_constraints}
- Currency and channel (EGP — Instagram, store, WhatsApp): {channel}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE MECHANIC: the offer type that fits my {reason} — %-off vs bundle vs gift-with-purchase vs free-delivery threshold — and why it beats a plain discount for my case.
2. THE MATH: the maximum discount my {product_and_margin} survives, the minimum order value if any, and the numbers customers will see.
3. THE FRAMING: how to present it (value framing, deadline, limited quantity) — honest urgency only; fake countdowns banned.
4. THE COPY: one announcement post + one reminder post (last day), each max 60 words, in my channel's language.
5. GUARDRAILS: who's excluded (existing-customer handling), how to end it cleanly, and the rule that stops discounts becoming the default price.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- If my margin can't carry a discount, say so and propose the value-add alternative instead.
- Deadlines must be real; the offer must actually end.
- EGP numbers, local occasions, and delivery norms where Egypt fits.
Note: Promise no guaranteed outcomes — state your assumptions explicitly and include one line noting these are planning estimates, not financial advice.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: mechanic + why it beats %-off, margin math with max discount, framing rules (honest urgency), announcement + reminder posts (≤60 words each), guardrails. Lead with THE MECHANIC.
