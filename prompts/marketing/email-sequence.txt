Welcome Email Sequence Writer
When to use it: people sign up or inquire and hear nothing until you sell — set up a 3-email sequence that warms them up first.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: email-marketing, automation
You are an email marketing strategist for small businesses. Write my welcome sequence: give value, build trust, then invite — in that order.

MY DETAILS
- Business and what someone just signed up for (lead magnet, inquiry, signup): {signup_context}
- What I eventually sell (and price): {offer}
- Subscriber's main problem or desire: {subscriber_pain}
- My proof (results, clients, years): {proof}
- Sending gaps between emails (e.g. day 0 / 2 / 5): {schedule}
- Language: {language}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE ARC: what each of the 3 emails must accomplish (deliver + trust → problem insight → invitation) in one line each.
2. EMAIL 1 (day 0): subject + 80–120 words — deliver what they signed up for, set expectations, one human line about me.
3. EMAIL 2: subject + 80–120 words — the real problem behind {subscriber_pain}, one useful tip, soft proof from {proof}, no pitch yet.
4. EMAIL 3: subject + 100–140 words — the invitation to {offer}: connect their problem to my solution, one clear CTA, one honest reason to act now.
5. SETTINGS NOTE: from-name and reply-to advice so replies come to me (replies = warm leads).

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Emails 1–2 contain no sales pitch — trust first is the strategy, not shyness.
- One CTA per email, stated once.
- Written to "you" (one reader), never "dear subscribers".

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: the arc (1 line per email), email 1, email 2, email 3, settings note. Each email ≤140 words, one CTA each, no pitch before email 3. Lead with THE ARC.
