Referral Offer Designer
When to use it: word of mouth is your best channel and you're leaving it to luck — design a referral program customers actually use.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: referrals, growth, loyalty
ℹ️ Planning guidance with stated assumptions — not financial advice.

You are a growth marketer for small businesses. Design my referral offer: the reward, the ask, and the tracking — simple enough to run on a WhatsApp and a notebook.

MY DETAILS
- My business, product, and average order value: {business_and_aov}
- My customers (who they are, how they talk about me): {customers}
- Margin headroom for rewards: {margin}
- Channels where referrals happen today (groups, family networks, offices): {channels}
- Currency: {currency}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE REWARD STRUCTURE: two-sided or one-sided (referrer vs new customer vs both), reward type (discount, cash back via Instapay/cash, gift, upgrade), and the exact amounts my {margin} survives.
2. THE TRIGGER MOMENT: when to ask for the referral (post-purchase high, after compliment, delivery moment) and the exact ask line in my customers' language.
3. THE MECHANICS: how the new customer proves who sent them (code, name mention, tagged message) — zero-apps version that works in Egypt.
4. THE PROMO: how customers learn the program exists — one-time announcement message + the receipt/packaging card text.
5. THE ABUSE GUARDS: the 3 exploits (self-referrals, fake accounts, refund loops) and the simple rules that block them.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Reward on confirmed, non-refunded purchases only — say it explicitly.
- Double-sided rewards convert best; if margin forbids it, say which single side to pay.
- Keep rules to 3 sentences a customer can read in 10 seconds.
Note: Promise no guaranteed outcomes — state your assumptions explicitly and include one line noting these are planning estimates, not financial advice.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: reward structure with amounts the stated margin survives, the trigger moment + exact ask line, zero-app mechanics, one-time announcement text, 3 abuse guards. Lead with THE REWARD STRUCTURE.

# Variables
- {business_and_aov}: required, business + average order value, e.g. "tutoring center, 800 EGP/month"
- {customers}: required, who your customers are, e.g. "parents in school WhatsApp groups"
- {margin}: required, margin headroom, e.g. "40%"
- {channels}: required, where word of mouth happens today, e.g. "parent WhatsApp groups"
- {currency}: The currency for all money figures (e.g. EGP).

# Example values: {business_and_aov}=tutoring center, 800 EGP/month per student | {customers}=parents in parent WhatsApp groups | {margin}=40% | {channels}=school parent groups, family networks | {currency}=EGP
