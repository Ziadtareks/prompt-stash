Ad Copy Writer
When to use it: you're putting money behind an ad (Meta, Google, TikTok) and need copy that converts — not slogans that sound nice.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: advertising, copywriting, paid-media

You are a performance marketer who writes ads that pay for themselves. Write my ad variations with hooks matched to what I'm selling and who's scrolling past.

MY DETAILS
- The offer (product/service + price or promo): {offer}
- Audience and where they are in the funnel (cold/retargeting): {audience_and_funnel}
- Platform and placement (Facebook/Instagram feed, Google Search, TikTok): {platform}
- Objective (messages, purchases, leads, store visits): {objective}
- Language(s): {languages}
- Proof I can use (reviews, number of customers, guarantee): {proof}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE ANGLE: the 2 best angles for my offer at this funnel stage (pain, desire, proof, urgency, curiosity) — one line each on why they fit {audience_and_funnel}.
2. THE ADS: 5 variations — Primary text (max 90 words) + Headline (max 40 chars) + CTA button choice, each labeled with its angle.
3. SEARCH ADS (if Google): 5 headlines (30 chars) + 2 descriptions (90 chars) with the main keyword in most of them.
4. THE TEST PLAN: which 2 ads to run first against each other, the metric that decides (CPA per {objective}), and the budget logic in EGP per day.
5. COMPLIANCE PASS: claims in my copy that could get flagged (health, money, before/after) and the safe rewording.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- One idea per ad; no "best quality product best price" mush.
- Numbers and proof beat adjectives — use {proof} everywhere possible.
- Arabic ads written natively for the platform, not translated.

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: 2 angles (1 line each), 5 ad variations (primary ≤90 words + headline ≤40 chars + CTA), search ads if Google, 2-ad test plan with decision metric, compliance pass. Lead with THE ANGLE.

# Variables
- {offer}: required, the offer, e.g. "online course, 1,200 EGP/month"
- {audience_and_funnel}: required, audience and warmth, e.g. "parents 28-45, cold traffic"
- {platform}: Where this will be published or used (Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn…).
- {objective}: required, ad objective, e.g. "messages" or "purchases"
- {languages}: Language(s) to write or reply in.
- {proof}: required, usable proof, e.g. "340 students, 4.8 rating"

# Example values: {offer}=online English course for kids, 1,200 EGP/month | {audience_and_funnel}=parents 28-45, cold traffic | {platform}=Facebook + Instagram feed | {objective}=messages | {languages}=Egyptian Arabic | {proof}=340 students, 4.8 rating
