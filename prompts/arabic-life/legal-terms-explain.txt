(توعية عامة فقط — خطوات المكاتب تتغير؛ اتأكد من الجهة الرسمية قبل التقديم.)

- The documents/terms on my papers I don't understand: {terms_list}
- My role in the transaction (buyer, seller, heir, tenant, grantor): {my_role}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TERM-BY-TERM TABLE: المصطلح | يعني إيه ببساطة | مين يصدره | امتى أحتاجه | خطره/حمايته ليا — for every term in {terms_list}, explained in simple Arabic with a short English gloss.

3. THE TRAPS: the classic mistakes with these exact documents (signing a توكيل with overbroad powers, unregistered rent contracts, إخلاء طرف signed before money moves) — and the one-line protection for each.
4. QUESTIONS TO ASK: the 5 questions to ask the lawyer/office before proceeding, phrased so a vague answer is a red flag.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Plain Arabic first, English gloss second — this is for someone standing in a government office line.
- Facts about document purposes only; no invented fees, office names, or current procedures — mark anything time-sensitive with [تأكد].
- End with the disclaimer: this is orientation, not legal advice.
Note: You are not a lawyer — say so plainly whenever legal topics come up, flag the points a licensed professional should review, and never state that a clause or position is definitely valid or invalid.

ARABIC STYLE
- Write the Arabic in Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى رسمية معاصرة) — formal/official register; Egyptian dialect would undermine the purpose. State nothing else about variety: MSA is fixed here.
- Use the conventional opening and closing for this document type (e.g. «تحية طيبة وبعد،» … «وتفضلوا بقبول فائق الاحترام والتقدير») and the correct honorifics for the recipient's rank.
- Never invent law numbers, article citations, official fees, or form names — write [تحقق] where one would go.
- Native phrasing only: no calques from English, no stiff translated word order, no padded formality. Short, precise sentences.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- خمسة أقسام: جدول المصطلح بمصطلح، خريطة خطوات المعاملة، الفخ الشائع لكل مستند، 5 أسئلة للمحامي/المكتب، تنبيه التكاليف بعلامات [تأكد]. ابدأ بجدول المصطلحات.
