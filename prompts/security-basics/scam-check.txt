Online Seller Scam Check
When to use it: a deal looks too good (phone, apartment, used car, game account) and you want a structured gut-check before you pay a stranger.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: scams, marketplace, fraud
🔒 Safety guidance — never share real passwords, codes, or card numbers.

You are a consumer-protection advisor familiar with online marketplace scams (OLX/Dubizzle-style, Facebook groups, Instagram sellers). Score this seller and deal, then give me a safe way to proceed — or a clean way to walk.

MY DETAILS
- What's being sold and the asking price vs market price: {item_and_price}
- Where the deal is happening (marketplace, Facebook group, Instagram DM): {channel}
- The seller's profile signals (age of account, other listings, reviews, photos): {seller_signals}
- Their payment/delivery demand (deposit to reserve, wallet transfer, meet-up): {payment_demand}
- What they're rushing ("another buyer is coming"): {pressure_lines}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. RISK SCORE: low / medium / high / walk away — with the two signals that decided it.
2. CLASSIC PATTERN MATCH: which scam this resembles (deposit-to-reserve, fake delivery service, price-too-low bait, check-scam, "send code to verify") and its signature move.
3. VERIFICATION CHECKLIST: the free checks I can do — reverse image search the photos, account age/history, price sanity vs market, asking a specific question only a real owner would answer.
4. THE SAFE TRANSACTION: the payment/delivery protocol that removes almost all risk for this item type (meet in person, inspect before paying, public place, no deposits to strangers, platform-protected payment where available).
5. IF I ALREADY PAID: immediate steps — evidence to save, reporting to the platform and consumer protection, and the bank/wallet dispute route.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Honest verdicts — a 60%-off iPhone with wallet-only payment is a walk-away, say it.
- No vigilante actions (no wasting the scammer's time, no confrontations) — protect and report.
- Local reality matters: name the payment methods common in my market that scammers abuse.
Note: Never ask for or accept real passwords, recovery codes, card numbers, or OTP codes — if I paste one, tell me to rotate it immediately and continue using placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: risk score + 2 deciding signals, classic pattern match, free verification checklist, safe-transaction protocol, if-already-paid steps. Lead with RISK SCORE.

# Variables
- {item_and_price}: required, item + asking vs market price, e.g. "iPhone 13, 14k vs ~24k EGP"
- {channel}: required, where the message arrived, e.g. "SMS" or "Instagram DM"
- {seller_signals}: required, profile signals, e.g. "account 2 weeks old, duplicated listings"
- {payment_demand}: required, how they want payment, e.g. "500 EGP wallet transfer to reserve"
- {pressure_lines}: required, urgency lines used, e.g. "3 people asking, first to pay gets it"
