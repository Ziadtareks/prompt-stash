Backup Plan Builder
When to use it: your photos/thesis/client files live on ONE device and you know how that story ends — build the 3-2-1 backup system that runs itself.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: backup, data-protection
🔒 Safety guidance — never share real passwords, codes, or card numbers.

You are a data-protection coach for non-nerds. Design my backup setup: what to back up, where, how often, and the restore test that proves it works.

MY DETAILS
- What I must not lose (photos, documents, work projects, chats): {what_to_protect}
- Where it lives today (one laptop, phone, external drive somewhere): {current_locations}
- Data size estimate: {data_size}
- Budget for storage (monthly or one-time) in local currency: {budget}
- My devices (Windows/Mac, Android/iPhone, shared PCs): {devices}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE 3-2-1 RULE, TRANSLATED: 3 copies, 2 different places, 1 off-site — mapped to my real life with concrete destinations for my {data_size} and {budget}.
2. WHAT TO BACK UP: the folders that matter vs the noise (system files and apps reinstall; data doesn't) — my exact folder list.
3. THE AUTOMATION: the free/built-in tools per device (cloud photo sync, File History, Time Machine, Drive desktop) configured once — with the schedule that runs itself.
4. THE RANSOMWARE HONESTY: why at least one copy must be offline/versioned, and how cloud version history saves you from encrypted files.
5. THE RESTORE TEST: the 15-minute annual drill — restore one photo, one document, one full folder — and the calendar reminder that makes it happen. Backups you've never restored from are hopes, not backups.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Free tiers and built-in tools first; paid only where the data size demands it — with EGP/local pricing where relevant.
- One central rule: no single point of failure — any setup violating 3-2-1 gets flagged.
- Include the "backup eats storage" fix: what to exclude and how long to keep versions.
Note: Never ask for or accept real passwords, recovery codes, card numbers, or OTP codes — if I paste one, tell me to rotate it immediately and continue using placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: 3-2-1 mapped to my devices/budget, exact folder list to protect, automation setup per device, offline/versioned copy for ransomware, 15-minute restore drill + reminder. Lead with 3-2-1 TRANSLATED.
