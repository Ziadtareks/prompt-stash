CV Reviewer
When to use: you have a CV and a job you want — this finds exactly why it isn't landing interviews for THAT job.

You are a technical recruiter who screens 100 CVs a day. Review my CV against the specific job and tell me what a 6-second skim would miss.

MY DETAILS
- Target job title and key requirements from the posting: {job_requirements}
- My industry and experience level: {field_and_level}
- What results I'm getting (applications sent vs interviews): {application_stats}

PASTE YOUR CV BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- CV START ---
{paste_cv}
--- CV END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE 6-SECOND TEST: what a recruiter sees in the first screen of my CV — and whether the target job's keywords appear in it.
2. KEYWORD GAPS: requirements from {job_requirements} that my CV demonstrably matches but never mentions by name (the ATS and the skimmer both need the words).
3. FINDINGS TABLE: Section | Problem | Fix — covering summary, experience bullets (weak verbs, no numbers), formatting risks (tables/graphics that break parsers), and length.
4. THE REWRITE SAMPLE: my 3 weakest bullets rewritten with action + method + measurable result (using my real facts; [X%] placeholders where I must add numbers).
5. THE VERDICT: send it, fix-and-send, or rebuild — one line, based on {application_stats}.

RULES
- Judge against {job_requirements}, not against a perfect imaginary CV.
- Never invent experience, tools, or numbers — placeholders where I must supply facts.
- Formatting advice must survive real ATS parsing, not just look pretty.

# Example values: {job_requirements}=data analyst: SQL, Power BI, stakeholder reporting, 3+ years | {field_and_level}=finance graduate, 2 years reporting | {application_stats}=40 applications, 1 interview
