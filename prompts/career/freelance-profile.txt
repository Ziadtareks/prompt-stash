Freelance Profile Builder
When to use it: your freelance profile (Mostaql, Khamsat, Upwork, Fiverr) gets views but no orders — rewrite it to convert.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: freelancing, platforms, profile
You are a freelance marketplace expert for MENA platforms. Build my profile so clients trust me in 10 seconds and message me instead of the next seller.

MY DETAILS
- Platform (Mostaql, Khamsat, Upwork, Fiverr): {platform}
- My service (what exactly I deliver): {service}
- My experience and proof (years, past work, results): {experience}
- My target client (startups, small shops, agencies — Egypt/Gulf/regional): {target_client}
- My starting price point in EGP/USD: {price_point}
- Languages I serve clients in: {languages}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TITLE: 3 profile/service titles matching what clients actually SEARCH for on {platform} — service words first, no "passionate".
2. OVERVIEW: first 2 lines that survive the preview cut (the problem I solve + proof), then 4–6 lines covering services, process, and why me. Written for {target_client}.
3. PACKAGES/GIG STRUCTURE: 3 tiers (basic/standard/premium) — what's in each, delivery days, and the pricing logic anchored on {price_point}.
4. PROOF SECTION: how to present {experience} with numbers, and which portfolio pieces to feature first.
5. TRUST BLOCKS: the FAQ answers and response-time promise that reduce client hesitation on {platform}.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Client-language first: describe outcomes ("sales page that converts") not tools ("I use Figma").
- Arabic platforms get native Arabic copy — no stiff translated feel.
- Prices must be consistent with {price_point}; never suggest underpricing below my floor.

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: 3 search-first titles, overview (first 2 lines survive the preview cut), 3 packages with delivery days, proof section plan, trust blocks (FAQ + response time). Lead with TITLE.

# Variables
- {platform}: Where this will be published or used (Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn…).
- {service}: required, the service you sell or priced, e.g. "brand identity package"
- {experience}: required, relevant experience + proof, e.g. "3 years, 12 delivered stores"
- {target_client}: required, your ideal client, e.g. "Egyptian/Gulf retail shops"
- {price_point}: required, starting price, e.g. "from 8,000 EGP"
- {languages}: Language(s) to write or reply in.

# Example values: {platform}=Mostaql | {service}=WordPress stores for small businesses | {experience}=3 years, 12 delivered stores, avg delivery 10 days | {target_client}=Egyptian/Gulf retail shops | {price_point}=from 8,000 EGP | {languages}=Arabic + English
