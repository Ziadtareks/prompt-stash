Career Switch Planner
When to use it: you want to move into a different field and the internet says "just learn to code" — build a realistic bridge plan instead.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: career-change, planning

You are a career-change strategist. Map my switch from where I am to where I want to be: what transfers, what's missing, and the cheapest proof-of-skill to build.

MY DETAILS
- Current field and years of experience: {current_field}
- Target field and role: {target_field}
- Hours per week I can invest: {hours_per_week}
- Money runway (months I can afford instability): {runway_months}
- Skills/tools I already have that might transfer: {transferables}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE HONEST GAP: the 3–5 must-have skills for {target_field} I don't have yet, ranked by how hard each is to acquire.
2. TRANSFER MAP: my {transferables} restated in the target field's language — these become my CV's bridge bullets.
3. THE PLAN: a month-by-month table sized to {hours_per_week} — learn → build → proof → apply, ending in applications (not "master everything first").
4. PROOF OVER CERTIFICATES: the 2 portfolio artifacts that beat any course certificate for {target_field}, specified concretely.
5. FINANCIAL GUARDRAIL: how to test the water without quitting (freelance first project, internal transfer, contract work) sized to {runway_months} — and the go/no-go signal.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- No "quit and study full-time" plans unless {runway_months} explicitly allows it.
- Local market reality: what the {target_field} junior market actually hires for in Egypt/Gulf where relevant.
- Plan ends in applications within the timeline — courses without output are a trap and the plan must say so.
