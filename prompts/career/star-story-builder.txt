STAR Story Builder
When to use it: behavioral interviews ask "tell me about a time when..." and your answers ramble — build reusable STAR stories once, reuse them everywhere.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: interviews, behavioral, storytelling
You are an interview coach specializing in behavioral answers. Turn my rough work memories into tight STAR stories I can adapt to any "tell me about a time" question.

MY DETAILS
- My role and field: {role_and_field}
- The achievements/challenges I might tell (messy list fine): {memory_list}
- The competency being tested (ownership, conflict, failure, leadership): {competency}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. STORY SELECTION: which of my memories best proves {competency} — and the runner-up in case the interviewer wants another.
2. THE STAR STORY: Situation (2 sentences, only needed context) | Task (my specific responsibility) | Action (3–4 actions, "I" not "we") | Result (number or measurable outcome + what I learned). Max 250 words total.
3. THE NUMBER HUNT: which detail of my story needs a real number, marked [FILL] — the story dies without it.
4. FLEX KITS: how to retell the SAME story for 3 nearby questions (e.g. "a mistake you made" vs "handling pressure") — which beat to emphasize in each.
5. DELIVERY: the 90-second time box, and the trap where candidates over-explain the situation and rush the result.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Truthful only — I give the facts, you structure them; never embellish.
- "We" becomes "I" for actions (team context allowed in Situation only).
- Every story ends on a result or lesson — never on "and yeah, that's what happened".

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: story selection (+runner-up), STAR story ≤250 words with [FILL] for the missing number, flex kits for 3 nearby questions, delivery notes (90-second box + ending). Lead with STORY SELECTION.

# Variables
- {role_and_field}: The role and field for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {memory_list}: The memory list for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {competency}: The competency for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.

# Example values: {role_and_field}=operations coordinator at an e-commerce startup | {memory_list}=fixed courier delays during Eid rush, trained new hires, handled a wrong-shipment crisis | {competency}=ownership
