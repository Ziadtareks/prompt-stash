LinkedIn Profile Fixer
When to use it: recruiters search LinkedIn daily and your profile is invisible — optimize it to be found, not just to look nice.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: linkedin, personal-branding
You are a LinkedIn profile strategist who knows what recruiters type into search. Rewrite my profile's search-facing parts so the right opportunities find me.

MY DETAILS
- My current role and the role I want to be found for: {current_vs_target}
- My top 3 skills and evidence for each: {skills_and_evidence}
- My audience language (recruiters in English / Arabic / both): {audience_language}
- What I'm open to (full-time, freelance, both): {open_to}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE KEYWORDS: the exact search terms recruiters use for {current_vs_target} in my market — these must appear in my profile.
2. HEADLINE: 3 options (max 220 chars) in the formula role | value | proof — never "Seeking opportunities".
3. ABOUT SECTION: 5–8 lines, first-person — who I help, how, proof numbers, and the "open to" line with contact direction. No buzzword soup.
4. EXPERIENCE SECTION RULES: how to rewrite each job's description (keyword-rich first line, numbers, tools named) — with one of my jobs rewritten as the example.
5. QUICK WINS: banner, skills ordering (top 3 = searchable), featured section, and the "Open to work" settings advice (recruiter-only visibility trade-off explained).

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Keywords come from how recruiters actually search in my {audience_language}, not from courses' buzzwords.
- Every claim ties to my {skills_and_evidence}; [FILL] where I must add numbers.
- Write it so a human likes it after the search engine finds it.

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: recruiter search keywords, 3 headline options (≤220 chars), About section (5-8 lines), experience rewrite rules + one worked example, quick wins. Lead with THE KEYWORDS.

# Variables
- {current_vs_target}: required, role you have vs role you want, e.g. "accountant → FP&A analyst"
- {skills_and_evidence}: required, top skills + evidence, e.g. "Power BI (built 6 dashboards)"
- {audience_language}: required, language your audience/recruiters use, e.g. "English recruiters in Gulf"
- {open_to}: required, what you're open to, e.g. "full-time" or "freelance + full-time"

# Example values: {current_vs_target}=accountant → FP&A analyst | {skills_and_evidence}=Power BI dashboards (built 6), forecasting (monthly close), SQL basics | {audience_language}=English recruiters in Gulf + Egypt | {open_to}=full-time
