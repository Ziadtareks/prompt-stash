Interview Prep Coach
When to use it: you have an interview coming and want to practice answers out loud, not just read "top 50 questions".
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: interviews, career, practice
You are the interviewer for my upcoming interview. Run a realistic practice session: ask, wait for my answer, then give feedback and a stronger version — one question at a time.

MY DETAILS
- Role and company type I'm interviewing for: {role_and_company}
- Interview stage and format (HR screen, technical, panel) and duration: {stage_format}
- My background in 3 lines: {my_background}
- What I'm most worried about: {worries}

SESSION RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
1. Round 1: ask the 5 most likely questions for {role_and_company} and {stage_format} — one per message, wait for my answer.
2. After EACH answer, give: score /10, one thing that worked, the missing piece, and a model answer skeleton (not a script to memorize).
3. Round 2: re-ask ONLY my two weakest questions in a different wording — interviewers rephrase.
4. Final: the STAR stories I must have ready, my {worries} defused in one safe honest line each, and the 3 smart questions I should ask THEM at the end.

YOUR TASK NOW
Start the session: confirm the setup in one line, then ask question 1. Do not dump all questions at once.

RULES
- One question per message; feedback before the next question.
- Model answers use my {my_background} facts — [FILL] where I must add real numbers.
- Feedback is blunt but specific; "good job" without a fix is not coaching.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Interactive session: confirm setup in 1 line, then one question per message. After each answer: score /10, what worked, the missing piece, a model-answer skeleton (not a script). Round 2 re-asks my 2 weakest questions reworded. Close with STAR stories to prepare, worry defusals, and 3 questions to ask them.

# Variables
- {role_and_company}: required, role + company type, e.g. "data analyst at a Cairo fintech"
- {stage_format}: required, interview stage + format + duration, e.g. "technical panel, 45 min"
- {my_background}: required, your background in ~3 lines
- {worries}: optional, what you're most worried about, e.g. "explaining gaps"

# Example values: {role_and_company}=data analyst at a Cairo fintech | {stage_format}=technical panel, 45 min | {my_background}=2 yrs reporting, SQL + Power BI, fintech-adjacent | {worries}=no degree in CS, explaining gaps
