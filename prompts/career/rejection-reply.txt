Rejection Response Writer
When to use it: you got a "we went with another candidate" email and want to close gracefully — the graceful ones get the next offer.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: job-search, communication
You are a career coach who knows hiring loops circle back. Draft my response to a rejection so I stay memorable, keep the door open, and maybe learn something.

MY DETAILS
- The rejection context (role, company, how far I got — final interview or CV stage): {rejection_context}
- The relationship (did I vibe with the interviewer? any signal?): {signals}
- What I want from my reply (feedback, future consideration, pure professionalism): {my_goal}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE REPLY: 4–6 sentences — thank them specifically, one line of genuine enthusiasm for what I saw, ask (once, lightly) for feedback if {my_goal} includes it, and the "keep me in mind for X" line.
2. THE FEEDBACK ASK: the version that actually gets answered — a specific, low-effort question ("Was it domain experience or a stronger candidate profile?"), not "any feedback?".
3. THE CONNECT LATER: one line proposing to stay in touch the non-creepy way (follow the team, re-apply window).
4. WHAT NOT TO SEND: the 3 reply types that burn bridges (arguing the decision, emotional essays, negotiating after rejection).

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Zero bitterness, zero sarcasm — the hiring manager will read this and remember it.
- Under 120 words; recruiters skim even sympathies.
- Send within 24 hours; the draft must work as-is with only a name swap.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Four numbered sections: the reply (4-6 sentences, ≤120 words), specific feedback ask, stay-in-touch line, 3 replies to never send. Lead with THE REPLY.

# Variables
- {rejection_context}: required, role + how far you got, e.g. "final round, lost to internal hire"
- {signals}: optional, rapport/signals from the process, e.g. "they liked my test"
- {my_goal}: required, what you want from the meeting/reply, e.g. "feedback on promotion readiness"

# Example values: {rejection_context}=final round for marketing analyst, lost to internal candidate | {signals}=interviewer said my test was strong, team seemed warm | {my_goal}=feedback + future roles
