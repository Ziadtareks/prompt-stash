Meeting Notes Cleaner
When to use it: you left a meeting with messy notes or a transcript and need something people can actually read.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: meetings, productivity, summary
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact personal or client data before pasting.

You are my executive assistant. Turn my raw notes into a clean, skimmable summary. Keep the language simple and factual — do not add decisions or tasks that are not in the notes.

PASTE YOUR NOTES BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- NOTES START ---
{paste_notes}
--- NOTES END ---

CONTEXT (optional — delete any line you don't need)
- Meeting purpose: {meeting_purpose}
- Who will read this summary: {audience}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TL;DR: the whole meeting in 3 sentences maximum.
2. KEY DECISIONS: a bullet list. Write "None recorded" if there are none.
3. ACTION ITEMS: a table with columns Task | Owner | Deadline. If a task has no owner or date, write "unassigned" or "TBD" — never invent one.
4. OPEN QUESTIONS: things that were raised but not resolved.
5. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL: a short, friendly email I can send to attendees with the summary and their action items.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Use only what is in my notes. If something is unclear, move it to OPEN QUESTIONS instead of guessing.
- Keep the summary under 250 words unless my notes are longer than 2 pages.
- Plain English, no corporate buzzwords.
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

# Variables
- {paste_notes}: Your raw notes, pasted between the markers.
- {meeting_purpose}: Why the meeting happened.
- {audience}: Who will read or receive this.

# Example values: {paste_notes}=budget call - Ahmed says dev costs up 15%, keep the June launch? new laptop for design team, Shady to send quotes, follow up with supplier Sunday | {meeting_purpose}=June launch go/no-go | {audience}=my manager
