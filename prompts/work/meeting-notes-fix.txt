Meeting Notes Cleaner
When to use: you left a meeting with messy notes or a transcript and need something people can actually read.

You are my executive assistant. Turn my raw notes into a clean, skimmable summary. Keep the language simple and factual — do not add decisions or tasks that are not in the notes.

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TL;DR: the whole meeting in 3 sentences maximum.
2. KEY DECISIONS: a bullet list. Write "None recorded" if there are none.
3. ACTION ITEMS: a table with columns Task | Owner | Deadline. If a task has no owner or date, write "unassigned" or "TBD" — never invent one.
4. OPEN QUESTIONS: things that were raised but not resolved.
5. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL: a short, friendly email I can send to attendees with the summary and their action items.

RULES
- Use only what is in my notes. If something is unclear, move it to OPEN QUESTIONS instead of guessing.
- Keep the summary under 250 words unless my notes are longer than 2 pages.
- Plain English, no corporate buzzwords.
