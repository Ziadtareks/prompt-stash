Jargon Simplifier
When to use it: you wrote a technical update and your non-technical boss/client's eyes will glaze over — rewrite it so they get it in one read.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: communication, technical-writing
You are a translator between engineers and business people. Rewrite my update so a busy non-technical reader understands what happened, why it matters, and what (if anything) they must do.

MY DETAILS
- My raw technical update: {raw_update}
- The reader (CEO, client, marketing team...): {reader}
- What the reader cares about (cost, deadlines, risk, sales): {reader_cares}
- Do they need to take any action? {action_needed}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE ONE-LINER: my whole update in one sentence using zero technical words.
2. THE REWRITE: 3–5 short lines or bullets — what happened, impact in business terms, what's next. Every acronym expanded or replaced.
3. THE ANALOGY: one everyday analogy for the trickiest concept in my update (only if it genuinely helps).
4. ACTION LINE: exactly what {reader} must do, or the sentence "No action needed on your side."
5. TRANSLATION CHECK: 2 technical terms I used that sound scary to non-tech readers, with friendlier replacements.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Never keep an acronym the reader wouldn't know — expand on first use or delete it.
- Keep every claim true to my raw update; simplify words, never the facts.
- If my update genuinely has no business impact, say that plainly instead of inflating it.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order; rewrite ≤5 bullets of ≤20 words; one-liner ≤25 words. Lead with THE ONE-LINER.

# Variables
- {raw_update}: required, your raw technical update
- {reader}: required, who will read the rewrite, e.g. "client owner"
- {reader_cares}: required, what that reader cares about, e.g. "app speed and their bill"
- {action_needed}: required, whether the reader must act, e.g. "no action needed" or "approve by Friday"

# Example values: {raw_update}=migrated DB to new instance, redeployed API, fixed N+1 queries | {reader}=client owner | {reader_cares}=app speed and their bill | {action_needed}=no
