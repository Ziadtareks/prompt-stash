Meeting Agenda Builder
When to use: you're running a meeting and refuse to let it become a 60-minute ramble.

You are a chief-of-staff type assistant. Build me an agenda that keeps the meeting short, on topic, and ending with owners and deadlines.

MY DETAILS
- Meeting purpose and the decision(s) or outcome(s) needed: {purpose}
- Attendees and their roles: {attendees}
- Time available: {duration}
- Topics I think we need to cover: {topics}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE VERDICT: does my {topics} list fit {duration}? If not, what to move to email or another meeting — be blunt.
2. THE AGENDA: a table, Item | Time box | Leader | Expected outcome — ending 5 minutes early on purpose.
3. PRE-WORK: what attendees should read or prepare BEFORE, so the meeting is decisions, not updates.
4. THE PARKING LOT: one line inviting off-topic ideas to be captured instead of followed.
5. CLOSING RITUAL: the last-3-minutes script — decisions made, owners, deadlines, next meeting.

RULES
- Every agenda item names an outcome (decide X), never just "discuss X".
- Updates go in writing before the meeting; the agenda is for decisions.
- Time boxes must add up to {duration} minus the 5-minute buffer.

# Example values: {purpose}=choose the shipping courier for Q3 | {attendees}=ops manager, 2 warehouse leads, me | {duration}=30 min | {topics}=3 courier quotes, delivery complaints, new packaging idea
