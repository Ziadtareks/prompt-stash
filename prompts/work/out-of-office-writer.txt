Out-of-Office Writer
When to use it: you're going on leave and need an auto-reply that protects your time without leaving people stranded.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: email, leave, communication

You are an executive assistant. Write my out-of-office message so it sets clear expectations, routes urgent matters to the right person, and needs no edits before I turn it on.

MY DETAILS
- My name and role: {my_details}
- Last day before leave / first day back: {dates}
- Who covers for me while I'm away (name + contact): {cover_person}
- Can I be reached for true emergencies, and how? {emergency_line}
- Audience (colleagues, clients, or both): {audience}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE MESSAGE: subject-free auto-reply body — dates, who to contact for what, emergency line, and a warm close. Max 80 words.
2. SHORT VERSION: a 2-sentence version for internal chat status (Slack/Teams away message).
3. URGENT ROUTING LINE: one sentence that filters "urgent" honestly (define what actually counts as urgent for my {audience}).
4. BEFORE-LEAVE CHECKLIST: the 4 things to set up before I go (delegate access, calendar block, pending replies flagged).

RULES
- Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- No oversharing (destination, reason for leave stay out unless I include them).
- Dates exactly as I wrote them — never ambiguous like "early next month".
- Professional but human; one friendly touch allowed.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Four numbered sections: MESSAGE (≤80 words), SHORT VERSION (2 sentences), URGENT ROUTING (1 sentence), CHECKLIST (4 items). Lead with THE MESSAGE.

# Variables
- {my_details}: required, your name and role, e.g. "Sara, sales support"
- {dates}: required, exact dates, e.g. "leave June 10-24, back June 25"
- {cover_person}: required, who covers you + contact, e.g. "Omar, omar@company.com"
- {emergency_line}: optional, how you can be reached for true emergencies, e.g. "phone for contract clients"
- {audience}: Who will read or receive this.

# Example values: {my_details}=Sara, sales support | {dates}=leave June 10-24, back June 25 | {cover_person}=Omar, omar@company.com | {emergency_line}=phone for clients under contract | {audience}=clients and colleagues
