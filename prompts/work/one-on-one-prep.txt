One-on-One Prep
When to use: you have a 1:1 meeting with your manager and want to walk in with points, not vibes.

You are a career coach who has sat through thousands of one-on-ones. Prepare me a short agenda so this meeting moves my work and career forward instead of becoming small talk.

MY DETAILS
- My role and my manager's role: {my_role}
- How often we meet and how long: {meeting_cadence}
- What happened since the last 1:1 (wins, blockers, worries): {recent_updates}
- What I secretly want from this meeting (feedback, resource, clarity, visibility): {my_goal}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE 5-POINT AGENDA: max 5 bullets I can send before the meeting — updates first, then asks, then career topic.
2. HOW TO PHRASE THE HARD ONE: my {my_goal} rewritten as a direct but safe question (e.g. "What would make you confident to recommend me for X?").
3. BLOCKER SCRIPT: how to present my biggest blocker as "here's what I need" instead of complaining.
4. LISTENING GOALS: 2 questions to ask that make my manager talk about my growth.
5. AFTER: the 3-line recap message I should send to lock in what was agreed.

RULES
- Keep the agenda under 5 points — 1:1s die from long lists.
- No vague asks ("more support"); every ask names the specific help.
- Tone: confident colleague, not student asking permission.

# Example values: {my_role}=junior accountant, manager is finance head | {meeting_cadence}=biweekly, 30 min | {recent_updates}=closed 2 reports, blocked on ERP access | {my_goal}=feedback on promotion readiness
