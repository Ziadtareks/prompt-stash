Reply Tone Rewriter
When to use it: you wrote a reply to an email or message, but the tone feels off — too harsh, too soft, or too stiff.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: email, communication, rewriting
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact personal or client data before pasting.

You are my communication coach. Rewrite my draft so it lands the way I intend, without changing the facts.

MY DETAILS
- Who I am replying to (boss, client, friend, stranger): {recipient}
- The tone I want (e.g. warm but firm, apologetic, neutral, confident): {desired_tone}
- The outcome I want from this reply: {goal}
- Any length limit (e.g. under 120 words): {length_limit}

MY DRAFT
{draft_reply}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. VERSION A: the rewritten reply, matching my tone and goal. Keep my facts exactly as written.
2. VERSION B: the same reply, 30% shorter, for when I need it tighter.
3. WHAT CHANGED: 3 quick bullets on what you adjusted in Version A (word choice, structure, softeners) and why.
4. RED FLAG CHECK: one line — does my draft contain anything that could be read as rude, passive-aggressive, or unclear? If yes, point to the exact sentence.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Do not add new promises, commitments, or facts that are not in my draft.
- Keep my greeting and sign-off unless they clash with the requested tone.
- If my goal and my draft clearly conflict, say so in one sentence and give the version that serves the goal.
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

# Variables
- {recipient}: Who this message is for and your relationship to them.
- {desired_tone}: How the message should land.
- {goal}: The outcome you want from this.
- {length_limit}: Maximum length for the output.
- {draft_reply}: Your current draft, pasted as-is.

# Example values: {recipient}=a client who delayed the project 3 weeks | {desired_tone}=firm but professional | {goal}=get written approval of the new timeline without losing them | {length_limit}=under 120 words | {draft_reply}=Honestly this is getting unprofessional, third delay, please confirm dates in writing today.
