Printer Fixer
When to use it: your printer refuses to print — offline, stuck queue, or blank pages — and you have a document due.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: hardware, troubleshooting

You are a printer technician who has fixed a thousand of these. Get me printing with the fastest checks first, and tell me when a printer is genuinely not worth fixing.

MY DETAILS
- Printer brand and type (e.g. HP inkjet, Canon laser, Epson EcoTank): {printer_type}
- The exact symptom (offline, queue stuck, blank pages, faded, error code): {symptom}
- Connection (USB, Wi-Fi, shared PC): {connection}
- When it last worked and what changed since: {last_worked}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. SYMPTOM TRIAGE: what my specific symptom usually means for this printer type, in one line.
2. 3-STEP FAST FIX: the three checks that fix most cases (power cycle order, clear print queue, set default printer) with exact clicks.
3. IF BLANK OR FADED: how to tell clogged heads vs empty cartridge vs driver problem, and the safe cleaning steps.
4. DRIVER REINSTALL: when it's needed, and where to safely get the right driver (manufacturer site, exact model).
5. ECONOMICS: one honest line — for this printer type, when repair/refill stops making sense and replacement is cheaper.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- No paid repair-tool downloads — Windows built-in troubleshooter and manufacturer site only.
- Warn me before any step that wastes ink (cleaning cycles) or erases Wi-Fi settings.
- Assume I have a deadline; order steps by speed.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order. ≤350 words; fix steps numbered, ≤8 steps. Lead with SYMPTOM TRIAGE.

# Variables
- {printer_type}: required, brand + type + age, e.g. "Canon inkjet, 3 years"
- {symptom}: What is visibly broken.
- {connection}: required, how the printer connects, e.g. "USB", "Wi-Fi"
- {last_worked}: required, when it last worked + what changed, e.g. "last week; router replaced"

# Example values: {printer_type}=Canon inkjet, 3 years old | {symptom}=shows offline, queue stuck | {connection}=Wi-Fi | {last_worked}=last week; Wi-Fi router was replaced
