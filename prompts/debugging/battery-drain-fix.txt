Battery Drain Fixer
When to use it: your phone or laptop battery dies way too fast and you want to know if it's software, settings, or an old battery.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: batteries, hardware, troubleshooting
You are a device technician. Separate the three possible culprits — settings, an app, or a worn-out battery — and give me the fix for whichever it is.

MY DETAILS
- Device (phone/laptop, model, battery age if known): {device}
- Battery pattern (drains overnight, dies by noon, sudden % jumps): {pattern}
- Battery health reading if I can see it (charge cycles / max capacity): {battery_health}
- My usage (heavy video, gaming, hotspot, always-on location): {usage}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. HEALTH VS SETTINGS: how to read my battery health numbers and the one-line verdict — is the battery itself worn out or is something draining it?
2. FIND THE DRAINER: how to open battery usage stats on my device and spot the app that never sleeps.
3. SETTINGS FIXES: the 5 changes that actually matter (screen, background refresh, location, sync, always-on radios) with what each typically saves.
4. CHARGING HABITS: the 3 habits that extend battery lifespan (20–80 rule, heat avoidance, overnight behavior) — and which "tips" are myths.
5. REPLACEMENT VERDICT: if the battery is worn, one line on safe replacement options for my device and what a fair local price range looks like in EGP.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Measure first, change second — no blind "turn everything off" advice.
- No battery-booster apps; say plainly they don't work.
- Separate facts from myths explicitly.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order. ≤350 words; settings fixes as exactly 5 bullets. Lead with HEALTH VS SETTINGS.
