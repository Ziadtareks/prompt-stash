Overheating Fixer
When to use it: your laptop gets hot, the fan screams, or it throttles and shuts down — you need the cause, not just "clean the vents".
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: hardware, laptops, troubleshooting

You are a hardware technician. Diagnose why my machine overheats, from the safe fixes I can do at home to the ones that need a workshop.

MY DETAILS
- Machine (laptop model, age, gaming or office use): {machine}
- When it overheats (gaming, charging, idle, video calls): {when_hot}
- Symptoms (loud fan, hot keyboard, sudden shutdowns, slow performance when hot): {symptoms}
- Environment (desk, bed, dusty room, no stand): {environment}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. NORMAL VS NOT: one line on what temperatures are normal for my use case, and the point where it's damaging.
2. SOFTWARE CAUSES: how to spot a runaway process or stuck fan curve (Task Manager / monitoring tool by name) before blaming hardware.
3. FREE FIXES: the physical basics — surface, airflow, dust — done safely (compressed air direction, what NEVER to open myself).
4. WHEN TO OPEN IT UP: which fixes need a workshop (thermal paste, fan replacement), the fair local price range in EGP, and how not to get overcharged.
5. PROTECTION: what to stop doing now so the machine survives until it's fixed (charging while in bed, blocking vents, gaming on soft surfaces).

RULES
- If the model or symptoms are ambiguous, ask up to 3 questions (age, when it overheats, shutdowns?) before the verdict.
- Safety first: never suggest opening a device under warranty or with a glued battery without saying so.
- No gimmicks (cooling pads verdict: honest one-liner).
- If shutdowns already happen, lead with "stop heavy use now".

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order. ≤350 words; free fixes ≤4 steps. Lead with NORMAL VS NOT.

# Variables
- {machine}: required, machine + age + use, e.g. "Dell G3, 4 years, gaming"
- {when_hot}: required, when it overheats, e.g. "20 min into any game"
- {symptoms}: required, symptoms, e.g. "loud fan, hot keyboard, sudden shutdowns"
- {environment}: required, usage environment, e.g. "on bed, dusty room"

# Example values: {machine}=Dell G3 gaming laptop, 4 years | {when_hot}=20 min into any game | {symptoms}=loud fan then sudden shutdown | {environment}=on bed, dusty room
