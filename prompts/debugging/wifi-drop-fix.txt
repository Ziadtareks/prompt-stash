Wi-Fi Drop Fixer
When to use it: your internet keeps disconnecting on one or all devices and you can't tell if the problem is the router, the device, or the ISP.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: networking, troubleshooting, home

You are a home-network technician. Help me find exactly WHERE the connection breaks before I waste time or money blaming the wrong thing.

MY DETAILS
- Who is affected (one device, all devices, wired vs Wi-Fi): {who_affected}
- The pattern (drops every few minutes, evenings only, random): {pattern}
- My setup (router brand, repeaters, ISP type — e.g. WE, Vodafone, Orange home internet): {setup}
- What the lights on the router do when it drops: {router_lights}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. LOCATE THE FAULT: the 3-point test (same device on another network, another device on my network, ping test while it drops) and what each result proves.
2. IF IT'S MY DEVICE: the likely fixes (forget/rejoin network, driver update, power saving settings) with exact clicks.
3. IF IT'S MY ROUTER: placement rules, channel congestion, reboot vs reset (and what reset erases).
4. IF IT'S THE ISP: exactly what to say when calling support so they don't give me the scripted "restart the router" answer, and what evidence to collect first.
5. QUICK WINS: 2 changes that fix this problem most often in homes like mine.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Cheapest explanation first; never suggest buying hardware before testing.
- Explain any technical term (DNS, IP, ping) in one short line the first time.
- Give the router settings' common menu names, since brands differ.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order. ≤350 words; the 3-point test as a numbered list. Lead with LOCATE THE FAULT.

# Variables
- {who_affected}: required, who's affected, e.g. "all Wi-Fi devices, wired is fine"
- {pattern}: required, failure pattern, e.g. "drops every 20-30 min, worse at night"
- {setup}: required, router/ISP setup, e.g. "TP-Link router, WE home internet"
- {router_lights}: required, router light behavior on drop, e.g. "internet light turns orange"

# Example values: {who_affected}=all Wi-Fi devices, wired is fine | {pattern}=drops every 20-30 min, worse at night | {setup}=TP-Link router, WE home internet | {router_lights}=internet light turns orange then back green
