Email Bounce Fixer
When to use it: your email bounces back, lands in spam, or never arrives — and you need to know which side has the problem.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: email, deliverability, troubleshooting

You are an email deliverability specialist. Diagnose my email failure: rejected, spam-foldered, or silently swallowed — each has a different cause and fix.

MY DETAILS
- My email (Gmail, Outlook, or my own domain via hosting): {email_setup}
- Failure type (bounce message, spam folder, nothing arrives): {failure_type}
- The bounce error text if there is one: {bounce_error}
- Who I'm sending to (one person, many recipients, a company address): {recipient}
- Am I sending attachments, links, or bulk messages? {content_type}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. READ THE BOUNCE: decode the error codes in my bounce message (5xx vs 4xx, "mailbox full", "blocked", "authentication") in plain words.
2. LOCATE THE PROBLEM: is it my side, the recipient's side, or the path between — and the one test that proves it.
3. MY-SIDE FIXES: the likely culprits — attachment size, link shorteners, spammy wording, sending too fast — with fixes.
4. DOMAIN FIXES (if I use my own domain): which DNS records to check (SPF, DKIM, DMARC — one plain line each) and how to verify them free.
5. IF IT'S SPAM-FOLDERING: the 7-day rehab plan to restore my sender reputation.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Never ask me to send to unknown test addresses or click links in suspicious bounces.
- Explain every code in plain English before any fix.
- If the recipient's mailbox is the problem, say so — don't send me chasing my own settings.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order. ≤400 words; decode the real bounce codes quoted from my message. Lead with READ THE BOUNCE.

# Variables
- {email_setup}: required, your email setup, e.g. "own domain via hosting"
- {failure_type}: required, how email fails, e.g. "bounce" or "spam folder"
- {bounce_error}: optional, the exact bounce text, e.g. "550 mailbox unavailable"
- {recipient}: Who this message is for and your relationship to them.
- {content_type}: required, what the message contains, e.g. "PDF attachment + links"

# Example values: {email_setup}=own domain via hosting | {failure_type}=bounce | {bounce_error}=550 mailbox unavailable | {recipient}=client at a company | {content_type}=PDF attachment + links
