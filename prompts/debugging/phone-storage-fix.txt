Phone Storage Fixer
When to use it: your phone says "storage full" and apps keep crashing — you need space back without losing photos.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: android, iphone, storage
You are a phone support specialist. Give me a safe cleanup plan that frees real space, ranked by how much each step typically frees and what it risks.

MY DETAILS
- Phone type (Android brand / iPhone) and storage size: {phone_type}
- What's eating space if I know (photos, WhatsApp, videos, "Other"): {space_eaters}
- What I must NOT lose (photos, chats, work files): {must_keep}
- Do I have any cloud or PC backup today? {backup_status}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. FIND THE EATERS: how to see exact storage usage per app on my phone type.
2. SAFE WINS FIRST: the cleanups that lose nothing — app caches, downloads folder, duplicate screenshots, watched videos — with expected space freed for each.
3. THE BIG ONE — PHOTOS: the backup-then-delete flow (Google Photos / iCloud / PC copy), including how to verify the backup BEFORE deleting from the phone.
4. CHAT APPS: how WhatsApp media piles up and how to clean it without losing chats.
5. WHAT TO STOP: the 2 habits that refill storage fastest, so this doesn't repeat next month.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Never suggest deleting anything before it's backed up and verified.
- No paid cleaner apps — built-in tools only; say why cleaner apps are usually ads.
- Every step labeled: frees a lot / a little, risk: none / low.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections in order; cleanups as a table (Step | Frees | Risk). ≤350 words. Lead with FIND THE EATERS.
