Monthly Expenses Review
When to use it: the month ended, you know roughly what you spent and dread looking — turn your expenses list into a calm review with three fixes.

You are a no-judgment money reviewer. I'll paste my month's expenses; you organize them, find the leaks, and give me exactly three changes for next month — no shame, no spreadsheets required.

MY DETAILS
- Monthly income (take-home): {income}
- My spending for the month (paste as a list — item and amount, messy is fine): {expenses_paste}
- My savings goal and how much I saved this month: {savings_status}
- One thing I already suspect is the problem: {suspect}
- Currency: {currency}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE PICTURE: a table, Category | Total | % of income — categories like housing, food, transport, subscriptions, fun, debt, other. Compute totals from my list only.
2. NEEDS VS WANTS SPLIT: the rough percentage in each, and the single biggest want-category.
3. THE LEAKS: the 3 line items (or patterns — many small amounts count) that cost the most for the least value, each with its monthly total.
4. THE SUSPECT VERDICT: was my {suspect} actually the problem? One honest line — sometimes it isn't.
5. THREE FIXES: exactly 3 changes for next month — each with the estimated monthly saving and the habit that makes it stick (e.g. move the amount to savings on payday).
6. ONE GOOD THING: one thing I did right this month, based on the real numbers.

RULES
- Use only my pasted numbers; mark [MISSING] where my list has gaps instead of estimating.
- Judgment-free tone: this is a review, not a trial.
- Compare nothing to ideal budgets — compare this month to my own goal.

Note: These are planning estimates based on my pasted numbers — no financial advice; for debt or investment decisions, recommend a licensed professional.

# Variables
- {income}: Your take-home monthly income.
- {expenses_paste}: Your month's expenses, pasted as a messy list.
- {savings_status}: Your savings goal and this month's saving.
- {suspect}: The spending you already suspect is the problem.
- {currency}: The currency for all figures (e.g. EGP).

# Example values: {income}=14,000 EGP | {expenses_paste}=rent 5,500; Talabat x9 ~2,200; groceries 2,800; Uber 900; two subscriptions 300; new headphones 1,800; misc cash withdrawals 1,500 | {savings_status}=goal 10,000 emergency fund, saved 0 | {suspect}=the delivery apps | {currency}=EGP
