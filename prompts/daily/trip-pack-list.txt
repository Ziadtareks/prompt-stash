Trip Packing List
When to use it: you always arrive and realize you forgot something important.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: travel, checklists
You are a seasoned travel planner. Build me a packing list for my exact trip — with quantities, split by bag, and a "leave at home" list so I do not overpack.

MY DETAILS
- Destination: {destination}
- Trip length in days: {days}
- Weather forecast or season: {weather}
- Trip type (beach, city, hiking, family visit, work): {trip_type}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. DOCUMENTS & MONEY: IDs, tickets, cards, insurance — the short list that ruins trips if forgotten.
2. CLOTHES with quantities for {days} days, adjusted for {weather} and {trip_type} — including the "one warm layer even if hot" rule where it applies.
3. TECH & CHARGERS: including destination-specific items (plug adapter type for {destination}, power bank).
4. HEALTH & SMALL: medicines, toiletries under any liquid limits, and 2 destination-specific items most people forget.
5. BAG SPLIT: what goes in carry-on vs checked (anything irreplaceable or rain-exposed goes in carry-on).
6. LEAVE-AT-HOME LIST: 5 things people commonly pack for this trip type that are not worth the weight.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Quantities per item, not vague lists.
- Repeat-wear logic: for trips over 4 days, do not multiply clothes by days linearly.
- Format as checkboxes so I can print it.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Six sections as checkbox lists: documents, clothes with quantities, tech, health/small, bag split, leave-at-home (5). No prose paragraphs. Lead with DOCUMENTS & MONEY.

# Variables
- {destination}: Where you are traveling.
- {days}: The days for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {weather}: The weather for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {trip_type}: The trip type for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.

# Example values: {destination}=Dahab | {days}=4 | {weather}=hot days, cool nights | {trip_type}=beach + light hiking
