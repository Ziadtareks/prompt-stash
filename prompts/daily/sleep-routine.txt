Sleep Routine Fixer
When to use it: you scroll until 2am and feel wrecked every morning.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: sleep, health, routines
⚠️ General wellness guidance — not medical advice.

You are a sleep hygiene coach. Build me a realistic evening routine around my actual schedule — no unrealistic "be in bed by 9pm" plans.

MY DETAILS
- The time I must wake up: {wake_up_time}
- The time I actually go to bed now: {bedtime_now}
- My main sleep disruptor (phone, late caffeine, racing thoughts, shift work): {main_disruptor}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TARGET BEDTIME: calculate the bedtime that gives me 7–8 hours before {wake_up_time}, and the realistic first step if my current {bedtime_now} is far from it (shift it by 30 minutes per week, not all at once).
2. THE WIND-DOWN HOUR: a timeline for the 60 minutes before target bedtime — what to do in each 15-minute block, ending with lights out.
3. THE DISRUPTOR PLAN: 2–3 specific counter-moves for {main_disruptor} (e.g. phone: charger lives outside the bedroom + app cutoff; caffeine: no coffee after 2pm).
4. BEDROOM CHECKLIST: the 4 environment factors that matter most (dark, cool, quiet, bed = sleep only) with a cheap fix for each.
5. CAN'T-FALL-ASLEEP PROTOCOL: exactly what to do if I am still awake after ~20 minutes (get up, dim light, boring activity, return sleepy) — never "just lie there".
6. 2-WEEK TRACKER: a simple table, Day 1–14, with columns Bedtime target met? | Wake feeling (1–5) | Note.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Realistic steps that survive a bad day.
- No sleep-tracking gadgets required.
- If my problem sounds medical (snoring gasping, insomnia for months), add one line advising a doctor visit.
Note: This is general guidance, not medical advice — tell me to consult a doctor before starting if I mention pain, a health condition, pregnancy, or medication.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Six numbered sections: target bedtime math, 60-minute wind-down in 15-minute blocks, 2-3 disruptor counter-moves, bedroom checklist (4), can't-sleep protocol, 14-day tracker table. Lead with TARGET BEDTIME.

# Variables
- {wake_up_time}: required, required wake time, e.g. "6:30 AM"
- {bedtime_now}: required, when you actually sleep now, e.g. "1:30 AM"
- {main_disruptor}: What hurts your sleep most.

# Example values: {wake_up_time}=6:30 AM | {bedtime_now}=1:30 AM | {main_disruptor}=phone in bed
