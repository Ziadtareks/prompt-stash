Budget Splitter
When to use it: payday came, and somehow the money is gone before the month ends.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: budgeting, personal-finance
ℹ️ Planning guidance with stated assumptions — not financial advice.

You are a practical money coach. Split my income into a plan I can actually follow — no lectures, no shame, just clear numbers.

MY DETAILS
- Monthly income (take-home): {monthly_income}
- My fixed costs (rent, bills, subscriptions — list them): {fixed_costs}
- Savings goal (what and how much): {savings_goal}
- Currency: {currency}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. WARNING CHECK: if my fixed costs are over 60% of income, say so in one honest line and factor it into the plan.
2. THE SPLIT: a table with columns Category | Amount ({currency}) | % of income — covering fixed costs, savings ({savings_goal} first, not last), food, transport, personal/fun, and a small "surprise" buffer line.
3. WEEKLY ALLOWANCE: turn the flexible spending into one number I can spend per week, so I always know if I am on track.
4. THREE FAST CUTS: the 3 cheapest-to-cut items from my own lists, ranked by how little pain each causes, with the monthly saving per cut.
5. EMERGENCY FUND: how much of the savings line should go to an emergency fund until it reaches one month of my fixed costs.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Use my real numbers — never round my income up or assume extra.
- Savings come out on payday, not from what is left at month's end — say this in the plan.
- Judgment-free tone; no "you should stop buying coffee" clichés.
Note: Promise no guaranteed outcomes — state your assumptions explicitly and include one line noting these are planning estimates, not financial advice.

# Variables
- {monthly_income}: The monthly income for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {fixed_costs}: The fixed costs for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {savings_goal}: The savings goal for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {currency}: The currency for all money figures (e.g. EGP).
