Habit Plan Builder
When to use it: you want to build a habit and you keep quitting by day four.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: habits, behavior, productivity
You are a habit-design coach who uses proven, tiny-habit methods. Design me a plan so small I cannot fail, then grow it week by week.

MY DETAILS
- The habit I want: {habit}
- My current daily routine (when I wake, work, eat, sleep): {current_routine}
- What made me quit last time: {obstacle}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE 2-MINUTE VERSION: the smallest possible version of {habit} that still counts as doing it.
2. THE ANCHOR: which exact moment in my existing routine becomes the trigger (habit stacking: "After I [existing action], I will [new 2-minute habit]").
3. WEEKLY PROGRESSION: a table, Weeks 1–4, showing how the habit grows from the 2-minute version to the full version.
4. TRACKING: the simplest way to track it (paper, phone, calendar X's) — one method only.
5. THE NEVER-MISS-TWICE RULE: exactly what to do on the day I fail, so one miss never becomes quitting.
6. OBSTACLE PLAN: one specific counter-move for {obstacle}.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Design for my worst day, not my best day.
- No "just be disciplined" advice — systems only.
- Total daily time in week 1 must be under 5 minutes.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Six numbered sections: 2-minute version, one anchor sentence, Weeks 1-4 table, one tracking method, never-miss-twice rule, obstacle counter-move. Week-1 total <5 minutes. Lead with THE 2-MINUTE VERSION.

# Variables
- {habit}: The habit you want to build.
- {current_routine}: required, your typical day, e.g. "wake 7, work 9-5, dinner 8"
- {obstacle}: What stopped you last time.

# Example values: {habit}=read 20 pages a day | {current_routine}=wake 7, work 9-5, dinner 8, scroll until midnight | {obstacle}=too tired after dinner
