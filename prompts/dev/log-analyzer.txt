Log Analyzer
When to use it: your app is failing and you have a wall of log lines — find the actual error, not the scariest-looking one.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: logging, debugging, incident-response
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact secrets and tokens before pasting.

You are an on-call engineer who reads logs for breakfast. Cut through my log noise to the root error and the trail that led to it.

MY DETAILS
- What's visibly wrong (500s, crash, hangs, wrong data): {symptom}
- When it started and what changed (deploy, traffic spike, config): {when_started}
- Roughly when in the log the failure happens: {time_window}

PASTE YOUR LOG BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- LOG START ---
{paste_log}
--- LOG END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE ROOT ERROR: the single line where things actually break (usually the FIRST error, not the last), quoted with its timestamp.
2. THE TRAIL: the 2–4 log lines before it that explain how the system got there.
3. WHAT IT MEANS: the failure in plain words — which component, what kind of failure (auth, network, data, resource).
4. NOISE FILTER: which repeating lines in my log are normal and safe to ignore next time.
5. NEXT STEP: the most likely fix OR the one additional log/metric I should capture if my excerpt is missing the cause.

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Quote real lines from my log with timestamps — never invent entries.
- If multiple distinct errors exist, identify the earliest and say the others look like consequences.
- If my excerpt is too small to diagnose, say exactly what to capture next (which lines, around which timestamp) instead of guessing.
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: root-error line quoted with timestamp, the 2-4 preceding lines, plain-words failure, noise filter, next step. Lead with THE ROOT ERROR.

# Variables
- {symptom}: What is visibly broken.
- {when_started}: required, when it started + what changed, e.g. "after yesterday's deploy"
- {time_window}: required, roughly when in the log, e.g. "around 14:03"
- {paste_log}: Your log output, pasted between the markers.

# Example values: {symptom}=checkout returns 500 for some users | {when_started}=after yesterday's deploy | {time_window}=around 14:03 | {paste_log}=80 lines including stack trace
