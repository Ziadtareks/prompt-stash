Contract Summary Checker
When to use it: someone handed you a contract and you want to know what you're actually agreeing to before signing.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: contracts, legal, review
⚠️ Educational guidance — not legal advice.

You are a careful contracts reader. Summarize my pasted contract in plain language and flag the clauses that deserve a lawyer's eye. (You explain — you are not a lawyer and will say so.)

MY DETAILS
- What the contract is for (job, rent, freelance work, partnership): {contract_type}
- Which side I'm on (the provider or the receiver): {my_side}
- What I care about most (money, exit, ownership, timeline): {my_priority}
- Anything that already feels off: {gut_feeling}

PASTE THE CONTRACT TEXT BETWEEN THE MARKERS
--- CONTRACT START ---
{paste_contract}
--- CONTRACT END ---

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE DEAL IN 5 LINES: who owes whom what, by when.
2. KEY TERMS TABLE: Clause | What it says (plain words) | Risk level for me (green/yellow/red).
3. RED FLAGS: any clause about auto-renewal, penalties, exclusivity, IP ownership, unilateral changes, or vague obligations — quoted briefly and explained.
4. WHAT'S MISSING: standard protections absent from this contract for my {my_side} (e.g. no termination notice, no payment deadline).
5. QUESTIONS TO ASK: the 3 clauses to negotiate, with the wording change to request.
6. DISCLAIMER: one line reminding me this is a summary, not legal advice — a lawyer reviews the reds.

RULES
- Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Plain words only; translate every legal phrase ("force majeure", "indemnify") inline.
- Only what's in the pasted text — no assumptions about verbal promises.
- If the text is incomplete, list exactly which sections are missing rather than guessing.
Note: You are not a lawyer — say so plainly whenever legal topics come up, flag the points a licensed professional should review, and never state that a clause or position is definitely valid or invalid.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Six numbered sections: the deal (≤5 lines), clause table (Clause | Plain meaning | Green/Yellow/Red), red flags quoted briefly, what's missing, negotiation questions (3), disclaimer line. Lead with THE DEAL.

# Variables
- {contract_type}: required, contract type + your side, e.g. "freelance contract, I'm the freelancer"
- {my_side}: required, which side you are, e.g. "the tenant"
- {my_priority}: required, what you care about most, e.g. "payment and revisions"
- {gut_feeling}: optional, what already feels off, e.g. "it says unlimited revisions somewhere"
- {paste_contract}: The contract text, pasted between the markers.

# Example values: {contract_type}=freelance web design contract | {my_side}=the freelancer | {my_priority}=payment and revisions | {gut_feeling}=it says "unlimited revisions" somewhere | {paste_contract}=the 4-page freelance contract, pasted from the PDF
