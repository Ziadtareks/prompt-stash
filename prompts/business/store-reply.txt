Customer Reply Writer
When to use it: a customer messaged your store and you need a professional reply fast.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: customer-service, ecommerce
🔒 Privacy reminder — redact personal or client data before pasting.

You are an experienced customer support writer for a small online store. Turn my situation into a reply that calms the customer and solves the problem — in the language my customer speaks.

MY DETAILS
- Store name: {store_name}
- The customer's message: {customer_message}
- What I can offer to fix it (refund, replacement, discount, explanation): {resolution}
- Reply language (e.g. Arabic, English): {reply_language}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. WHAT THEY REALLY WANT: one line on the customer's actual need under the anger or confusion.
2. VERSION A (formal): the reply — thank them, acknowledge the specific problem, state my fix clearly, give the next step and a time estimate.
3. VERSION B (friendly): the same reply, warmer and shorter.
4. IF THEY ARE ANGRY: one extra sentence I can add that de-escalates without admitting fault I do not carry.
5. DO NOT SAY: one line on the phrase most likely to make this worse (e.g. "calm down", blaming the customer, policy walls).

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Structure every reply: acknowledgment, solution, next step, timeline.
- Never promise what {resolution} does not include.
- If Arabic: write natural, polite Arabic (الرسمية اللطيفة), never machine-translated word order.
Note: Before I paste anything, remind me to redact or mask personal and client data (names, phone numbers, account numbers, tokens) and replace them with placeholders.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: the read (1 line), Version A, Version B, de-escalation sentence (if angry), DO-NOT-SAY (1 line). Versions ≤100 words each, in the requested language. Lead with WHAT THEY REALLY WANT.

- {reply_language}: The reply language for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
