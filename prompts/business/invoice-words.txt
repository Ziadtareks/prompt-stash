Invoice Writer
When to use it: you finished work for a client and need a clean invoice with the right wording.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: invoicing, freelance, payment
ℹ️ Planning guidance with stated assumptions — not financial advice.

You are a freelancer's assistant who has sent a thousand invoices. Draft my invoice so it looks professional and gets paid faster.

MY DETAILS
- My business / my name: {your_business}
- Client name: {client_name}
- What I did (items or hours): {items_list}
- Currency: {currency}
- Payment due in this many days: {due_days}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE INVOICE: a clean layout with these fields filled or marked [FILL]: invoice number suggestion, today's date placeholder, my details, client details, and an items table (Description | Qty | Price | Total).
2. TOTALS: subtotal, a tax line marked [ADD IF APPLICABLE], and the final total in {currency}.
3. PAYMENT TERMS: 2 polite sentences stating the {due_days}-day deadline, accepted payment methods placeholder, and late-fee wording that is firm but friendly.
4. THANK-YOU LINE: one sentence thanking the client that invites more work.
5. OVERDUE REMINDER: a separate 3-sentence reminder message I can send if the invoice passes {due_days} days unpaid.

RULES
- Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- No fake tax numbers, addresses, or IDs — mark anything I must fill with [FILL].
- Keep the wording polite but unambiguous about the deadline.
- The layout must be copy-paste ready as plain text.
Note: Promise no guaranteed outcomes — state your assumptions explicitly and include one line noting these are planning estimates, not financial advice.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Five numbered sections: header block, items table, totals block, terms block (≤4 lines), overdue reminder (≤3 sentences). Plain-text layout, copy-paste ready. Lead with THE INVOICE.

# Variables
- {your_business}: required, your business/name + contact details
- {client_name}: The client's (or counterparty's) name.
- {items_list}: required, what was delivered or worked on
- {currency}: The currency for all money figures (e.g. EGP).
- {due_days}: required, payment deadline in days, e.g. "14"

# Example values: {your_business}=Sara Design Studio | {client_name}=Cafe Rivea | {items_list}=logo design, 3 social media templates | {currency}=EGP | {due_days}=14
