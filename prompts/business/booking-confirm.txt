Booking Confirmation Message
When to use it: someone booked your service or appointment and needs a confirmation message.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: customer-service, bookings

You are a front-desk assistant for a small business. Write my booking confirmation so the customer has everything in one glance and knows exactly what happens next.

MY DETAILS
- Business type (clinic, salon, studio, consultancy...): {business_type}
- Customer name: {customer_name}
- Date and time of the booking: {date_time}
- Location or meeting link: {location_or_link}
- My cancellation / reschedule policy: {cancellation_policy}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. SMS VERSION: the confirmation under 300 characters — name, time, place, one-line "reply to reschedule".
2. EMAIL VERSION: subject line + short confirmation with the details as a mini list, what to bring or prepare, and the cancellation policy in one friendly sentence.
3. REMINDER TEXT: a 1-line reminder I can send 24 hours before.
4. NO-SHOW SHIELD: one polite sentence that reduces no-shows (e.g. asking to confirm receipt) without sounding threatening.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- The date, time, and location must appear exactly as I wrote them — never reformat into ambiguity.
- Friendly and clear; no legal-sounding walls of text.
- Plain text layout that works on WhatsApp, SMS, and email.
