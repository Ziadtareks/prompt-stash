Refund Apology Letter
When to use it: you must refund a customer and you want to keep their trust (and maybe their business).
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: customer-service, refunds
You are a customer-experience writer. Draft my refund message so the customer ends the conversation feeling respected — not processed like a ticket number.

MY DETAILS
- Customer name: {customer_name}
- What went wrong: {what_went_wrong}
- Refund amount and original payment method: {refund_amount}
- Store name: {store_name}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS: 3 clear subject lines (no "URGENT!!", no vague "Regarding your order").
2. THE MESSAGE, in this order: greet by name, own what went wrong in one honest sentence (no excuses, no blaming the courier), confirm the refund of {refund_amount} to the original payment method, state the realistic timeline for it to appear, offer one goodwill gesture, and close with an invitation to come back.
3. TIMELINE HONESTY: one line reminding me what to check before I promise a date (bank processing times), so I do not promise 24 hours when it takes 5 days.
4. WHAT NOT TO WRITE: the 3 phrases that ruin refund letters (blame-shifting, "as per our policy", "this is not our fault").

RULES
Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Calm and human, not groveling and not robotic.
- One sincere apology, early — never repeat it five times.
- Under 150 words for the message itself.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Four numbered sections: 3 subject lines, the message (≤150 words: own it → refund details → timeline → gesture → invite back), timeline honesty (1 line), 3 banned phrases. Lead with SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS.

# Variables
- {customer_name}: required, customer name, e.g. "Khaled"
- {what_went_wrong}: required, what failed for the customer, honest, e.g. "wrong size shipped twice"
- {refund_amount}: required, amount + destination, e.g. "1,200 EGP to the original card"
- {store_name}: required, store/business name, e.g. "Nour Home"

# Example values: {customer_name}=Khaled | {what_went_wrong}=wrong size shipped twice | {refund_amount}=1,200 EGP to the original card | {store_name}=Metro Wear
