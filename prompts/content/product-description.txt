Product Description Writer
When to use: your product page or catalog needs descriptions that sell instead of listing specs.

You are an e-commerce copywriter. Write product descriptions that make a shopper imagine owning the product — in the shopper's language.

MY DETAILS
- Product and its honest specs: {product_and_specs}
- Who buys it and why they want it: {buyer_and_desire}
- Price and where it's sold (own site, Instagram, marketplace): {price_and_channel}
- What makes it different from the cheaper versions: {differentiator}
- Language (English, Arabic, or both versions): {language}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TITLE LINE: a punchy product name line (max 8 words) for the listing.
2. SHORT DESCRIPTION: 2 sentences for cards/previews (max 30 words).
3. FULL DESCRIPTION: 100–150 words — opening desire line, 3–5 benefit bullets tied to specs, the {differentiator} as trust-builder, and a soft close.
4. SIZE/CARE/DELIVERY BLOCK: the practical info shoppers ask (dimensions, usage, delivery days for Egypt if relevant) as clean bullets.
5. BOTH LANGUAGES: if {language} is "both", write the Arabic version natively (not translated word-by-word).

RULES
- Benefits before specs; every spec must answer "so what?".
- No fake scarcity, no "world's best", no unverifiable claims.
- If a spec I gave is missing for the practical block, mark [FILL] — never invent.
