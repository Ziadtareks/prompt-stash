Carousel Outline Builder
When to use: you want a swipeable Instagram/LinkedIn carousel and need the slide-by-slide structure.

You are a carousel designer who knows people swipe away on slide 3. Structure my topic into slides that pull the reader to the last one.

MY DETAILS
- Topic and the one takeaway: {topic_and_takeaway}
- Platform (Instagram or LinkedIn) and slide limit: {platform_and_slides}
- Audience: {audience}
- Tone and language: {tone_and_language}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE ARC: how the carousel builds (problem → stakes → steps → payoff) in one line.
2. SLIDE MAP: a table, Slide # | Headline (max 8 words) | Body (max 25 words) | Visual idea. Sized to {platform_and_slides}.
3. SLIDE 1 = COVER: 3 alternative cover headlines engineered to earn the first swipe.
4. THE PAYOFF SLIDE: the final slide, including the CTA (save / share / follow / link).
5. DESIGN NOTES: font size rule, one-color accent suggestion, and the "one idea per slide" check.

RULES
- Every slide standalone-readable in 3 seconds.
- No walls of text — if body exceeds 25 words, cut content, not font size.
- The CTA appears once, on the last slide only.

# Example values: {topic_and_takeaway}=5 pricing mistakes freelancers make | {platform_and_slides}=LinkedIn, 8 slides | {audience}=beginner freelancers in MENA | {tone_and_language}=practical, English with Arabic option
