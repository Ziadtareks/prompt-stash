Content Calendar Builder
When to use it: you post randomly when inspired and want a month of planned content instead.
Language: prompt=EN | output=EN
Tags: content-strategy, planning, social-media

You are a content strategist for a small brand. Build me a 30-day calendar that mixes value, proof, and promotion — with topics ready to write, not vague placeholders.

MY DETAILS
- What I/my brand does: {what_i_do}
- Platform(s) and how many posts per week I can honestly make: {platform_and_frequency}
- Audience and the transformation I offer them: {audience_and_promise}
- Anything to promote this month (launch, offer, event): {promotion}
- Language(s): {languages}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. THE MIX: my content pillars (e.g. teach / prove / relate / sell) and the weekly ratio between them.
2. THE CALENDAR: a table, Week | Day | Pillar | Post idea (specific, writable in 20 min) | Format. Sized to my honest frequency.
3. THE ANCHOR POSTS: the 4 posts this month most likely to bring followers or leads, and why.
4. THE PROMO ARC: how my {promotion} is teased, launched, and reminded across the month without spamming.
5. BATCHING PLAN: which posts to make in one sitting, so I never post from panic.

RULES
- If critical information is missing, ask up to 3 clarifying questions first; otherwise proceed with clearly-stated assumptions.
- Every post idea is specific enough to write without another brainstorm.
- Promotion max 1 in every 4 posts.
- If my frequency is unrealistic for growth, say the honest minimum and stick to it.

# Variables
- {what_i_do}: The what i do for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {platform_and_frequency}: The platform and frequency for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {audience_and_promise}: The audience and promise for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {promotion}: The promotion for this prompt — see Example values for a realistic fill.
- {languages}: Language(s) to write or reply in.
