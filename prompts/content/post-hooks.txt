Post Hook Generator
When to use it: your posts are good but nobody reads past the first line — fix the hook.
Language: prompt=EN | output=user-choice
Tags: copywriting, hooks, social-media

You are a direct-response copywriter specializing in first lines. Write me opening hooks that create a gap the reader must close by reading on.

MY DETAILS
- The post topic and its core point: {topic_and_point}
- Platform (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook): {platform}
- Audience: {audience}
- Tone (bold, relatable, expert, contrarian): {tone}
- Language: {language}

YOUR TASK — answer in this exact order:
1. TEN HOOKS: 10 first lines in my {tone} — at least 2 curiosity hooks, 2 number/stat hooks, 2 story-opening hooks, 2 contrarian hooks, 2 pain-point hooks.
2. TOP 3 RANKED: which 3 fit my topic best and one line each on why they'll work for {audience}.
3. FIRST PARAGRAPH: for my #1 hook, the 2 sentences that follow it and keep the promise.
4. ANTI-CLICHÉ CHECK: flag any hook I should avoid because it's overused on {platform} in 2025–2026.

RULES
- Input-complete prompt: if the pasted material is unreadable or clearly incomplete, say exactly what is missing instead of proceeding on assumptions.
- Hooks are max 12 words and never clickbait — the post must deliver what the hook promises.
- No "🚀 Excited to announce..." energy unless I ask for it.
- Write in {language}; keep cultural references natural, not translated.

If the requested output language is Arabic: use Egyptian dialect for casual/social content or Modern Standard Arabic for formal contexts — state which you used — and write natural native phrasing a native reader would actually say, never a stiff translation from English.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Four numbered sections: exactly 10 hooks (2 each of curiosity/stat/story/contrarian/pain), top-3 ranking with reasons, first paragraph for #1, anti-cliché check. Lead with TEN HOOKS.

# Variables
- {topic_and_point}: required, topic + its core point
- {platform}: Where this will be published or used (Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn…).
- {audience}: Who will read or receive this.
- {tone}: The voice to write in.
- {language}: Language (and dialect) for the output.

# Example values: {topic_and_point}=freelancers underprice because they quote hourly | {platform}=LinkedIn | {audience}=Arab freelancers | {tone}=contrarian | {language}=English with Arabic option
